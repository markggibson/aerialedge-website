Back by popular demand…circus school to brighten up your school holidays! 
Whether you do one day or the full five days of aerial, acrobatic and object manipulation training, it’s guaranteed to be the most fun thing you do all week!

No previous experience is required, the activities are adjusted for each individual so that everyone is capable of achieving some cool aerial and acrobatic moves, step by step. You can see videos of Youth Circus fun at www.aerialedge.co.uk 

If you’ve done no physical activities other than school PE lessons, you’ll discover a whole world of exciting things to do.

If you’ve already done dancing, gymnastics, acrobatics, parkour or trampoline, circus activities will actually develop those skills further, while you learn new arts.
And what WILL you learn? Oh, ALL KINDS of awesome tricks, moves and sequences! Flying trapeze and trampoline feature in every morning session. Afternoons are about aerial arts including trapeze, silks, hoop and rope, along with acrobatics, acrobalance, parkour and tumbling.  You’ll come home joyful and jubilant. What's not to love? 

Check out the video at www.aerialedge.co.uk to see what's on offer. We make sure there are enough teachers to split the students into small groups so that every student gets the support they need. 

There’s a mini-showing on Friday afternoon so that family and friends can see what you’ve been doing.
You can book specific days at £60 each or come for the whole week at £250, saving £50. Suitable for all abilities, ages 7-18. Parents can watch from the reception coffee area, or drop and go.

- This week-long opportunity usually fills up so early booking is recommended.

- If you enjoy this, you will love Youth Circus classes which run on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays, and you can perform in the end-of-term show if you want!
