MAKE GRAVITY YOUR CO-STAR

DYNAMIC WORKSHOPS ARE HARD TO BEAT

If hanging still isn’t scratching your aerial itch, Freya Pellie’s dynamic workshops are your ticket to the next level: big beats, quick transitions, and split-second moments where momentum feels like magic.

These sessions are all about timing, power, and play. You’ll learn how to harness the swing instead of fighting it – catching the exact moment to move, release, or let the apparatus take you somewhere unexpected. Think flowing beats, punchy direction changes, and that delicious swoop when everything lines up just right.

Freya is known for making the impossible feel doable. She breaks down complex dynamic pathways into clear, confidence-building steps, giving newer beat-seekers strong foundations while offering seasoned aerialists plenty of spice and speed to chew on.

Whether you’re here to level up your dynamic toolkit or simply find out what “whoosh” really looks like in your body, you’ll leave with fresh movement ideas, sharper timing, and a better feel for the rope or hoop as a true dance partner.

TAKE FLIGHT ON POLE

NOTHING STAYS STILL IN ELKE'S WORKSHOPS

It’s time to leave the floor behind, pole people! Join aerialist Elke Bucher for a pair of dynamic fly pole workshops designed to lift you up whether you’re just beginning your pole journey or already pulling off advanced tricks.

These sessions dive into the essentials – clean technique, safe progressions, and the distinct challenges of working on a free-spinning, rubber-coated pole. You’ll explore creative entries, learn how to work with the spin, borrow anti-dizziness tips from ballet and aerial practice, and craft combinations tailored to your own skills.

With an aerial repertoire spanning pole, hoop, and hair hanging, Elke has performed throughout the UK and internationally and champions curiosity over perfection.

Their teaching is about building control, celebrating experimentation, and discovering what you’re capable of in the air.

Expect breathtaking shapes, challenging spins, and a chance to fully immerse yourself in the unique flow and freedom of fly pole.

Beginners workshop: No prior pole experience needed – just general fitness and a sense of adventure.

Intermediate/Advanced workshop: Experience on shiny or Chinese pole and comfort with inverts will help you unlock deeper work.

STAY COSY, STAY SAFE

ADD AN EXTRA LAYER THIS WINTER

Winter is upon us, so please be prepared for Youth Circus classes with layers of clothing on both tops and bottoms – and definitely don't leave home without warm socks! It's way better to have too many layers than too few.

While we have a couple of heaters, and we do warm-up exercises, it's cold in the space – so save the shorts, T-shirts and bare feet until spring.

Please come with leggings, joggers, and long-sleeved fleeces, jumpers or hoodies. It's not just for comfort, it's also for safety because cold muscles and ligaments are more susceptible to injuries.
