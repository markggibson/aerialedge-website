READY TO BOOK OCTOBER EDGE PATHS?

30MIN TIL IT OPENS: STEP BY STEP INSTRUCTIONS BELOW

Booking for paths opens tomorrow

Hey Mark,

You can reserve the tracks you want to train at the October Edge from TODAY at 1pm, so be ready. The schedule is below.

There are 9 spots in each workshop, so some will get booked up quicker than others. Be prepared with alternatives if you don't manage to get your first choices for each time slot.

We recommend booking the discipline that you want the most, rather than doing it in order of time slots.

Here's what to do:

Your October Edge pass gives you four credits. You'll use one for each of your paths, which will reserve your spot in the same workshop at the same time each day.

1.⁠ ⁠Login to Teamup ready for the booking system going live at 1pm on Saturday 18th October.

2.⁠ ⁠⁠Open the calendar and go to Friday 31st October. 

3.⁠ ⁠⁠Find the first workshop you want to book and click to open, just as you would for any class or workshop.

4.⁠ ⁠⁠Click to book your workshop.  That will reserve your sessions in that time slot for Friday through Sunday (it may say 'Register for Group' which just means the workshops are grouped into a path through the weekend).

5.⁠ ⁠⁠This will use one of the four credits that comes with your October Edge pass. 

6.⁠ ⁠⁠Repeat until you have booked all four workshop paths. 

Whichever arts you train across the four days, it's going to be a fantastic experience and you'll get great benefit from it.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder
