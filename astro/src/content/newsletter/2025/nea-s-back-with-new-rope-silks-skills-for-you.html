MAKE THE MOST OF THE FRINGE

YOUR BEST CHANCE FOR INSPIRATION

Hopefully you're taking some time to catch some shows at the Edinburgh Fringe – we're so lucky to have some of the best circus performers in the world on our doorstep for these few weeks.

As well as being a great day and/or night out, it's fantastic inspiration. It makes you think about your own practice and consider how aerial and acrobatic skills can become performance when you add elements such as surprise, theatrical skills, storytelling and sometimes costume.

Technical skills are one thing, performance is a whole different set of abilities that can be learned by anyone who has the inclination. We'd love you to consider preparing an act for the next Open Rig Night, and for Youth Circus students, think about the Performance Programme.

You won't know you love it until you try it...and then there's no looking back.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

NEA HERE AGAIN

TEACHING HER NEW SKILLS AT OCTOBER EDGE

Nea is back from Barcelona next week and all set to bring her newly acquired knowledge to your classes – and the October Edge.

After training rope and silks up to seven hours a day at La Bonita, she’s brimming with new techniques and new ways to explain existing ones.

She says: "I have expanded my knowledge on dynamics. On silks, people can definitely expect even more confusing sequences!"

As well as rope and silks, the October Edge gives you the chance to try new arts and make great progress in your favourites – set your goals with your instructor and go for it over the three days. You'll do workshops in the same four disciplines every day so you can really focus.

Other arts include flying trapeze, static trapeze, straps, aerial hoop, aerial hammock, acrobatics and more. Kerri might be persuaded to do tippy hoop or hoop-silks...you can get a taster of those in her workshops on Saturday 30th August, and tell us which you like best.

SOMETHING FOR EVERYONE

ALL CIRCUS FORMS ARE HERE

Fringe is not over yet! There is one more full week of shows and some venues are running right up until Monday 25th so there is still plenty of time to book in some incredible circus.

For Aerial Dance Lovers...

Imago | Corporeal Imago

The Canadian company are in Edinburgh with this titular show and it is a must for anyone who love aerial dance, moody lighting and doubles aerial.

A beautiful duet performed on the floor and in the air on multiple rope loops, the variety of basing and flying positions and transitions creates a mesmerising tangle of limbs.

13:00 at Assembly Roxy until August 24th (not Mondays). Book here

For Theatre Lovers...

Hamlet | New York Circus Projects

Have you ever watched a production of a Shakespeare play and thought, "Hey, this could use some circus"? Well, what a great idea! This show is for you!

Featuring some of the most talented international circus artists out there and receiving strong reviews all round, this production is great when you want circus with some strong storytelling architecture to it. There are other Hamlets at the Fringe – don't buy the wrong tickets...that would be a tragedy....

19:00 at Central Assembly Roxy until August 24th (not Mondays). Book here

For Cabaret Lovers...

La Clique | La Clique London

Sometimes the classics are just the best and La Clique demands to be seen. La Clique is the original sexy circus cabaret that 21 years ago delivered an entire new genre to the Fringe.

This anniversary show has a revolving cast of iconic talent including some of the original cast as well as newer fabulous faces. Best suited to adult audiences, be a part of circus cabaret history and fight for a ticket to this often sold-out show.

Multiple shows a day, at the World Famous Spiegeltent St Andrew's Square until August 24th (Not Mondays or Tuesdays). Book here

Sophie's Surprise 29th | Three Legged Race Productions

If you are up for a wild circus night out that is reminiscent of the house parties of your student days, then this nostalgia-laden, hilarious, participatory circus cabaret features some of London's finest artists and enough 90s references to truly make 'Fetch' happen.

Another show that is best for adults, this is also best booked in advance due to its popularity.

21:45 at Underbelly Circus Hub until August 23rd (Not Mondays). Book here
