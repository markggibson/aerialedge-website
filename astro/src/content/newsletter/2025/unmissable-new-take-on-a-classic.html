NEW TAKE ON FAMILIAR TALE

CLASSIC CHARACTERS REVIVED THROUGH CIRCUS

Step into an unmissable reimagining of a Charles Dickens classic Christmas tale with our Foundation Course students’ graduation ensemble show.

Through a combination of circus, dance, and theatre, A Christmas Carol³ is performed by three talented students who bring Scrooge’s famous spirits to life with more magic than ever before, though through aerial storytelling, acrobatics, and flying trapeze.

Pegg: the Ghost of Christmas Past

Pegg is a contemporary performance artist, who trained as a dancer at Northern School of Contemporary Dance. She embodies the Ghost of Christmas Past with fluid, ethereal grace, having embraced circus experimentation with enthusiasm. She weaves dance and aerial technique into her creative approach, and is excited to continue experimenting with circus arts after the course.

Grace: the Ghost of Christmas Present

Grace found circus, inexplicably, a year ago through the unlikely route of academia. Despite originally training hoop, she’s discovered a love for verticals, increasingly specialising in silks. She brings presence and character to the Ghost of Christmas Present – and a surprise non-circus talent!

Mardie: the Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come

Mardie takes on the Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come with striking, acrobatic intensity thanks to her focus on acrobatics and now hula hoop. She studied show acrobatics in Essen, Germany, before coming to the Aerial Edge course.

Director Michelle Ross is a circus artist with a master’s in theatre and performance practices. She crafts bold, boundary-pushing work that blends circus imagery with theatrical conventions to heighten and expand the meaning and narrative of her work.

And that’s what’s shaped the ambitious vision of a well-known tale, and sends it soaring in a fresh direction.

MOVE SMARTER, NOT HARDER

GENTLE FOUNDATIONS THAT MAKE DYNAMICS EASY

If the word dynamic makes you think “too fast,” “too hard,” or “not for me,” these are the workshops that prove otherwise.

Dynamic aerial isn’t just big beats and circus-level power. It’s about learning how to use momentum so your body works with the rope or hoop, not against it. Think of it as finding the swing, the rhythm, and that effortless feeling when the apparatus starts doing some of the work for you.

Freya Pellie specialises in helping people unlock this. She takes the mystery (and the trepidation!) out of dynamic movement and teaches it in small, confidence-building steps. You’ll learn how to generate gentle beats, control your timing, and ride momentum smoothly long before anything gets big or spicy. Most people leave surprised by how accessible it feels once they understand the technique.

Expect playful swings, satisfying transitions, and that lovely “ohhh, that’s how it works” moment when everything clicks into place. There will be laughter, experiments, and zero pressure to go higher or faster than you want.

Whether you’ve been on a rope or hoop for years or you’re just beginning to explore new ways of moving, these workshops will give you:

• Simple foundations for people who’ve never tried dynamics

• Safe progressions that build strength and confidence

• Clear explanations of beats, timing, and momentum

• Creative transitions that make your aerial feel more fluid

• Options for all levels – you choose how dynamic you go

If you’re curious, cautious, or just craving something fresh in your aerial practice, Freya will guide you into dynamic movement in a way that feels achievable, fun, and surprisingly freeing.

Give it a try…you might find your new favourite way to fly.

KERRI BOOKED FOR EASTER EDGE

START THINKING ABOUT YOUR 2026

It’s a good time to look ahead to 2026, because we have some brilliant news: Kerri is officially returning for Easter Edge, bringing back her hugely loved tippy hoop and aerial hammock sessions.

Many people cornered Kerri at the October Edge to ask, “Are you coming back for Easter?”

Well… yes. Absolutely yes.

Kerri has a unique gift for breaking down dynamic, sometimes intimidating skills into playful, achievable progressions that make you feel both safe and daring at the same time. Her blend of precision, creativity and joyful teaching energy means people leave her workshops feeling stronger, braver and excited to try more.

Easter Edge runs Friday 3rd April til Monday 6th, offering four immersive days to sharpen your skills, explore different disciplines, and train alongside expert coaches. Pick your focus – hoop, trapeze, hammock, and more – and take your training to the next level.

And that energy is exactly what we’re pouring into next year’s full training programme.

If you’re ready to dive deeper, our full-time courses include:

• 1-Year Professional Development Course – polish your chosen disciplines, create solo and ensemble acts, and graduate with a showreel. Classes are Monday to Thursday – you can self-train on Fridays or use the long weekend to chill or work.

• 4-Month Foundation Course – full-time, immersive training with performance opportunities and a showreel. Classes are Monday to Friday.

• 4-Week Circus Intensive Course – Starts the same as the Foundation Course with a broad range of arts and you have a chance to perform if you want.

• Pro-Track Training – flexible schedules tailored to your goals.

Whether you’re exploring new skills or preparing for a professional path, Easter Edge and our full-time courses offer the perfect playground. Spaces are limited, so book early!
