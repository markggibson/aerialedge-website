STRAPIFY YOUR LIFE

AERIAL ACE JASON IS GUEST INSTRUCTOR AT OCTOBER EDGE

You’ve got an incredible opportunity coming your way at October Edge, our three-day circus convention! We’re bringing in Jason Brügger, an internationally acclaimed aerialist best known with his signature style which combines strength, elegance, and emotional expression.

Jason isn’t just an amazing performer, he’s an inspirational instructor who knows what it takes to turn obstacles into strength.

When he achieved his dream of qualifying for the prestigious National Circus School in Montreal, he developed Morbus Menière, an ear disease that caused dizziness and hearing loss – check out the video above to find out about that journey.

'Give up circus'

His trainers told him to give up circus, it was too dangerous. He didn’t. He stuck to his dreams, graduated, and has worked with international circus companies as ever since, as well as winning Switzerland’s Got Talent with his beautiful straps artistry.

Jason says: “I love teaching more than performing. A lot of circus people struggle with something – I share my story to say that no matter what people tell you, it is possible.”

Scottish roots

Swiss-born and raised, Jason is super-excited for his first visit to Scotland – not least because his great-grandparents were from Glasgow!

He said: “I feel really connected to the music! The pipes and drums give me goosebumps all over my body, it makes me really emotional and I don’t know where that comes from – it must be in my genes.”

This is your chance to train with one of the most creative and inspiring artists in contemporary circus – don’t miss it!
