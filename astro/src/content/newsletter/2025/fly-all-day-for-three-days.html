COURAGE IN MOTION

PERFORMERS ARE ALWAYS WINNERS

Yesterday, our four-week intensive students took the stage for their first informal showings and the Foundation Course students joined in – giving us a sneak peek of the performance experience usually reserved for Open Rig Night.

For some, it was their very first time performing, and for most people nerves are always part of the picture. But as always, those who step up discover the same truth – performing is worth every heartbeat of anxiety.

Hoop, silks, trapeze, and acrobatics – each act was full of skill, determination, and courage. It may be rare for any performer to come off stage feeling their act was perfect, but that’s never the point. What matters is the growth, confidence, and thrill of sharing your abilities with a friendly audience.

Over the years, some students have hesitated, refused point blank to perform in front of a live audience, or backed out before the show. Yet those who take the leap always gain more than just applause. They leave braver, more self-assured, and ready for the next challenge.

Every act, no matter the experience level, is a triumph of personal courage, resilience and the joy of daring to perform – great reasons to celebrate stepping into the spotlight.

When will you take the leap? You have time to create and rehearse an act for Open Rig Night on Saturday 25th October. Sign up below.

And for youth students, you can join the Performance Programme and be in the show in December. More on that below.

Mark Gibson

FLY ALL DAY FOR THREE DAYS

FOUR TRACKS AT THE OCTOBER EDGE

Wanna fly all day long? Well you can at the October Edge! Flying trapeze is in EVERY time slot on the schedule because Hamish will be back from his teaching tour in London, Doretto will still be here and Jonah is up for it. As for Gabe, it depends on the state of his knee by that stage.

Of course you'd be mad to do six hours of fly training for three days, for a couple of reasons...

First, your hands will be shredded. Second, you'd be missing out on other opportunities considering all the other arts at this circus festival. In each time slot there's also an aerial art, a vertical and an acro option.

But hey, it's up to you. We're just here to give you choices. One or two of the fly tracks can be dedicated to intermediates if we see that enough of you book tickets (we know who you are 👀).

With a clutch of top instructors, you get plenty of catchers. Maybe you want to spend the time between now and the October Edge prepping to catch a trick or two out of lines, or to catch a bigger trick.

You can see from the videos that Hamish is nailing his double on the big rig in the big smoke. Doretto's not had much time to train himself since Gabe hasn't been around to pull lines but his solid acrobatic background meant he could jump straight into a twisty belt yesterday and throw a layout full twist.

The beauty of training consistently three days in a row is that it's all fresh in your body and you raise the chances of hitting your goal.

How can you resist?

ANYTHING IS POSSIBLE

CHANGE YOUR LIFE IN 4 WEEKS OR LESS

It's always impressive and inspiring to see incredible acts after just four weeks of training on the Circus Intensive – and Alena, Lena and Laura were outstanding at their end-of-course showings yesterday.

In all courses, there's a range of capabilities in the students. Some may have good experience of certain arts, others may never have done circus before.

But no one is good at everything – and that's a great leveller. It means that people collaborate and encourage each other. Someone's strength helps someone else's challenge as they all study a wide range of arts at the beginning of the course.

By the time it comes to choosing your favourite art to create an act, you have already gained a lot of skills, and support.

Full-time students train alongside part-timers on the Pro-Track option and pro artists or instructors who are in for Open Training.

It's all part of the magic that results in performances that shine even after such a short time.

CORE BLIMEY

WHY CHINESE POLE IS UNIQUE

Edd's back to take over straps class and re-start Chinese pole on the week beginning Monday 15th September.

At least you've been able to keep up your straps practice, but now you can take on the unique triumphs and challenges of the pole – which is unlike any other circus discipline.

It may only be a single pole but it's a limitless gravity-defying playground, combining strength, agility, and precision in every movement.

It challenges your body in new ways, builds incredible upper-body and core power, and develops coordination that transfers to all other aerial and acrobatic skills.

If you want a discipline that’s visually striking, physically demanding, and totally addictive, Chinese Pole is it.

PLAY OR PERFORM?

CHOOSE THE PATH THAT SUITS BEST

Regular Youth Circus classes or the Performance Programme – what’s the difference and which is the right fit for you?

Both offer incredible circus skills and tons of fun. Regular classes are all about variety, play, and confidence-building – ideal for kids who want to explore everything without the spotlight.

The Performance Programme adds choreography and shows for those who love creating and performing – and those students even wowed crowds at Zippos Circus recently. They were able to jump in at short notice because they already had a solo aerial act that had been created for their own show.

Whichever you choose, you will learn amazing skills, make friends, and have a blast.

SOAR WITHOUT SCORES

Have you thought about our new Youth Acrobatics Classes? Here, kids flip, balance, and soar without the pressure of competition, judges and scores.

Think all the fun of gymnastics, but with a circus twist. In this class there's no hard discipline, just creativity and confidence.
