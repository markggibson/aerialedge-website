FIND YOUR FLIGHT

FROM A FLICKER OF CONFIDENCE TO FULL-ON COURAGE

Some kids coming to Youth Circus camp next week will launch themselves off the trapeze and grin like they’ve discovered magic. Others will stumble onto something inside them that they didn’t know was there – a flicker of confidence that changes the way they move.

Adults feel it too. In one class you might push your body into shapes it’s never made before. Another class might give you a quiet thrill, discovering balance and control you didn’t know you had.

It’s all good. It’s all you – no rules, no comparisons. Circus is mischief, wonder, discovery, and that rush when gravity lets you have a moment of your own.

And sometimes, the rig calls you to share those moments with an audience at Open Rig Night. Are you in?

UNLOCK YOUR AERIAL POTENTIAL

BOOK EDGE TRACKS FROM SATURDAY (MAKE ONE JJ's)

Booking for your October Edge tracks opens at 1pm next Saturday – and whether or not you consider yourself a straps artist, you should seriously consider our guest instructor Jason Brügger’s workshops.

JJ has a way of finding your cool, that special thing about you that can transform your aerial practice.

And straps is arguably the best aerial art for conditioning so even if you don’t want to train it as an art in its own right, it can do you nothing but good.

JJ says: “Every single person has something special, their own strength. For me it’s easy to see potential in someone, I really try to encourage them to push into certain directions and make something amazing happen.

“I look at each individual, deciding what that person can do really well, and we dig deeper into that.”

What makes Jason’s October Edge workshops special is his approach:

  • Start with solid technique so you know what’s possible with your body

  • Find your unique strengths and develop your own style

  • Build confidence because circus should be about growth, not competition

A serious ear condition wasn't the only adversity JJ overcame to take straps artistry beyond his big top performances into millions of homes by winning Switzerland’s Got Talent.

As well as coaches at the prestigious National Circus School in Montreal, Canada, saying he should give up because the disease was affecting his balance so badly that training was dangerous, there were other voices challenging his dedication.

He says: “I experienced a lot of jealousy and competition in Montreal and that stopped my body from reaching its full potential in my artistry. I developed that after graduation.

“So I believe it's really important to encourage everyone to be confident about trying things out when I'm coaching.”

So how about it? Ready to try things out, with one of the top-flight aerialists working today?

BOUNCE & FLIP

TWO TRACKS, ONE POWERHOUSE COACH

If you’ve met Sam, you know his energy is contagious – and this October Edge, you get double the dose. He’s leading both the acrobatics and trampoline tracks, combining precision, power, and circus creativity.

“My approach to trampolining,” Sam says, “is to strengthen everyone’s foundational movements and focus on technique and control, allowing us to build up to creating a routine.

“If you have skills already that you want to explore I'm more than happy to share lots of drills.”

Oh – and remember: you want long sleeves, and no zips or buckles on the tramp! The sleeves are to protect you and the rest is to protect the trampoline.

On the acrobatics track, Sam draws from his background in tumbling, cheerleading, and floor gymnastics. “Over the three days, we’ll explore front, side, and back tumbling, creating different lines on the tumble track,” he says. “There will be partner work and synchronisation too – really bringing that circus style to life.”

Handstands might also make an appearance if there’s demand among people in the acrobatics track.

“It depends who’s in the room and what their goals are,” Sam explains. “We’ll condition the body for safe alignment, get the wrists and shoulders ready, and find the entries and exits that suit you best. Everyone has their own preferences – we’ll find yours.”

FROM TRAINING TO TRIUMPH

THE FRIENDLIEST STAGE IN CIRCUS IS CALLING YOU

It’s time to plan your moves – midnight this Friday is the deadline to submit your form to perform at Open Rig Night. If you’re coming to watch, expect inspiration, jaw-dropping moves, and moments that will stick with you all week.

Even if you’re not ready to commit to performing, take a look at the signup form. It helps you think through details like whether to start off-stage or the rig, in the spotlight or shadows, when to begin the music, and so on. Planning ahead makes your moment on the rig smoother and more enjoyable.

The form requires you to submit an mp3 file of your music because internet streaming is unreliable and no one wants the song to stop in the middle of a performance. If you need help with music, just ask us.

Now’s the time to try something wild, test a new combo or experiment with a friendly audience.

GET DOWN TO LIFT OFF

DISCOVER HOW DANCE COMPLETES YOUR CIRCUS TOOLKIT

What if your aerial work didn’t just defy gravity – it danced with it? Amber Dollin’s new contemporary dance class invites you to explore the art of connection: between ground and air, movement and meaning, strength and softness.

Drawing on contemporary techniques and floor work, Amber helps you find fluidity, spatial awareness, and creative transitions that feed directly into your aerial practice.

It’s about building artistry from the ground up, and unlocking new pictures in the air.

And – bonus! – it’s part of our Circus Fitness family. That means it is:

  • Just £7.50 with an eight-class Circus Fitness pass

  • One hour long – short and sweet

  • One of the foundational skills for circus disciplines

So if you’ve wanted to move with more flow, feeling, and intention, this is where you can start.

TWIST & PLAY

INVENTIVE ROPE WORKSHOP WITH FOX

Fox McLavin isn’t your typical aerialist – if there is such a thing. Fox calls himself an aerial gremlin, and nine years in the air with a fear of heights have taught them to turn challenges into inventive movement.

In this one-off workshop, you’ll explore weird and wonderful rope shapes, creative wraps, and lasso sequences that start in the body and evolve on the apparatus. Twists, drops, and frame work are all on the menu, giving you the freedom to experiment and invent your own movement style.

Designed for intermediate to advanced rope enthusiasts, Gremlin 101 focuses on fluidity, originality, and listening to what your body wants to do.

Pre-requisites: hiplock from scissor kick, ankle wraps, flamenco grips, and catchers.
