## ONE LAST BOW BEFORE THE BREAK

BEFORE HEAD OFF FOR  MINCE PIES

What stood out in last weekend’s performances was the care that went into them. Care from the instructors for what the students took on to the stage, care from the students for their act and their family and friends seeing them, and care for each other.

That doesn’t happen by accident. It’s the result of instructors who take their responsibility seriously without taking themselves too seriously. People who know when to challenge, when to support, and when to step back and let young performers discover what they can do.

I’m hugely proud of the youth students for showing up with talent and nerve, and of the team who guide them so thoughtfully week after week.

As we head into the Christmas break, I hope you all get some rest, some joy, and plenty of play in whatever form suits you best outside of circus. Thank you for being part of this community — whether you train, watch, support, teach, or cheer from the sidelines.

Have a good one! We're open til Sunday for your last circus fix until after the New Year.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

## DON'T LET GRAVITY GET YOU DOWN

LET FREYA SHOW YOU HOW TO USE IT IN YOUR FAVOUR

There’s a moment in aerial where the work drops away and movement starts carrying you. That’s the territory Freya Pellie can take you to.

Her rope and hoop dynamic workshops are focused on momentum, timing and efficient pathways through the air. It's about making what you already do travel further, faster, and with more ease.

Dynamic Rope plays with beats, turns, hand-slide transitions, simple releases and punchy combos. It's all grounded in clear mechanics so you understand why things work and how to build your own.

Dynamic Hoop takes familiar shapes and sets them in motion. Expect refined beats, alternate grips, dynamic switches, drops and smooth ways of turning exits into powerful entries.

Both workshops are designed for Improver/Intermediate students and anyone curious about expanding their aerial range.

The principles translate across disciplines too, making this a valuable tune-up beyond rope or hoop – and definitely into flying trapeze (which we know you're big into).

Freya teaches on our Foundation and Professional Development programmes and is known for making dynamic work feel playful, intelligible and confidence-building.

## TWO STAGES, ONE BIG WEEKEND

YOUTH CIRCUS TAKES THE FLOOR

Last weekend belonged to our Youth Circus students. The clips above are just a few of the highlights – we'll share more in newsletters and on Instagram Stories.

On Saturday night, the Performance Programme stepped into the spotlight with Midwinter, an ensemble piece shaped by months of training, collaboration and shared decision-making. It involved moving together, listening to each other, taking risks as a group. The kind of work that only happens when trust is doing as much lifting as strength.

Then on Sunday afternoon, the energy shifted but the courage stayed the same.

Our regular Youth Circus classes took over the space for their end-of-term showings. Short sequences in their favourite skills, each performance was different. Some bold, some careful, some quietly proud. All of them a step forward.

These two kinds of performance matter in different ways - one asks for unity and long-term commitment. The other is more about exploration, confidence-building and finding your feet on stage.

Both are excellent circus, and both very much deserve all the applause they got.

Your Youth Circus membership will roll on automatically through the two-week Christmas break. The cost of the missed classes is part of the calculation – membership is based on 48 weeks of classes in a year, divided by 12 months.
