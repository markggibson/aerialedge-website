THE BUZZ OF BEGINNINGS

BRIGHT NEW THINGS ARE STARTING

If you're quick and decisive, you have just enough time to join the next Foundation or Circus Intensive Course which both start on Monday.

There's something extra special about these, because it's the start of the educational year, and they begin together, so students from both courses train together for the first month.

That's when there's a broad range of arts being taught, and everyone's got different abilities in each art – no one is good at everything. It's a great leveller which naturally leads to cohesive groups with mutual support and encouragement.

People's strengths – not just in circus – come into play and new ones emerge.

It's the same newness buzz as Youth Circus begins again after the summer break. Friendships begin or are revived, along with the spirit of adventure and exploration.

Let the playing commence!

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

READY FOR PERFORMANCE?

CHOOSE WHICH CLASS TO TAKE

Finally! The summer hols are over and Youth Circus is back!

Now is a good time to decide if you want to switch from regular classes to the Performance Programme – you could be like Ivy, Sukayna, Cora and Sayuri performing in a real big top last week for a big audience when Zippos Circus was in town!

The four were able to jump at the chance because they were stage-ready at the drop of a hat. That's because they'd already created fully fledged acts during the term leading up to the Performance Programme's last show, The Life of a Wild Thing.

Performance Programme is open to ages 10-18 for students who enjoy a slightly more structured learning framework than the regular classes. It's all still playful and fun exploring aerial and acrobatic techniques and tricks.

It's just that there's a show to create for December, which provides natural motivation to progress as the students collaborate to turn their ideas into their ensemble performance.

If you'd like to try Performance Programme, email info@contact.aerialedge.co.uk

Play with other Scots youth aerialists

Aerialists aged 15–18 can join the first Scottish Youth Circus Exchange on Sunday 17th August — a one-day action-packed event bringing together youth circus students from across Scotland.

The event from 11am-6pm at the Dunblane Centre includes:

• 🎪 Training in rope, dynamic hoop, contortion and object manipulation

• 🎪 A creative, performance-focused session

• 🎪 Info from the National Centre for Circus Arts

• 🎪 Meeting and collaborating with other young aerialists from across Scotland

The event costs £20 and will be fully supervised by experienced circus coaches and staff. If you have questions, email sharisse@aerialdance.co.uk

Otherwise, pack your lunch, fill your water bottle, get into your aerial training kit and take a pair of trainers because there might be tightwire.

Scottish Youth Circus Exchange

Sunday 17th August | 11am-6pm | Dunblane Centre, Dunblane

Cost: £20

OVERCOME OVERWHELM

JUST GO STRAIGHT TO THESE FRINGE SHOWS

It’s August in Scotland and that means that the Edinburgh Fringe is calling out to you…an opportunity to see some of the best circus shows from around the world. But how to choose when the progamme is so daunting and there is just so much on offer?

Fortunately, Vee is a Fringe aficionado and has some weekly top pics for anyone wanting to journey through to Edinburgh. Here we go!

FOR ACROBATICS SEE...

The Genesis | Copenhagen Collective

What can you do with 17 acrobats?! Wonder no longer – just go see Copenhagen Collective, a new ensemble company in Edinburgh for the first time.

Expect three-highs, four-highs, hand-to-hand and and more with this smash hit show, which has been touring around the world non-stop since it was launched.

We have even heard that one of the ensemble, David, trained with Aerial Edge on the Commonwealth Youth Circus programme way back in 2014!

12:30 at Assembly Main Hall until 25th August (not 11th or 18th). Book here

Ten Thousand Hours | Gravity and Other Myths

A huge hit at the Fringe last year, Australian company Gravity and Other Myths are back, so if you missed this show last year, or even if you didn't, take this chance to catch this astonishing ensemble acro company.

18:00 at Assembly Main Hall until 25th August (not 11th or 18th). Book here

FOR AERIAL SEE...

Delusional - I Killed a Man | Diana Salles

A contemporary aerial solo show that embarks on a trans woman's journey, Delusional has already received strong audience reviews for its beauty and poignancy.

15:05 at Summerhall until 24th August (not Tuesdays). Book here

FOR THE FAMILY SEE...

The Unlikely Friendship of Feather Boy and Tentacle Girl | Vee Smith & Sadiq Ali

Shameless self-promotion! The show is only doing a short run, so catch it while you can. Exploring the friendship of a boy who wants to fly and a girl who wants to be a monster, this Chinese Pole and aerial performance is part of the Made in Scotland Showcase. Suitable from age eight.

11:25am at Assembly Roxy until 17th August (not Wednesdays). Book here
