## BOLD PLAY SPARKS GREATNESS

YOUNG STARS SHOWING US THEIR TALENTS

Tonight’s a big night! Our Youth Circus Performance Programme students take the stage with Midwinter – a show born from months of creativity, teamwork, and play which is courageous and collaborative.  I hope you’ll come to see them bringing their ideas to life, it’s always inspiring.

Tomorrow morning, we shift gears but keep the performance energy, with the end-of-term showings for all the Youth Circus students. These are more relaxed and less structured. This is a chance for everone to step up, share their favourite skills in an informal way, and enjoy perform in a supportive space.

Without these students and our fantastic, energetic instructors, there wouldn’t be performances like this to share. And without family and friends, the students wouldn’t have an audience to perform to – some come along and enjoy the moment with them.

Mark Gibson

## JUMP-START YOUR AERIAL MOMENTUM

DYNAMIC WORKSHOPS TO SET YOU UP

Kick off the new year with something special – Freya Pellie’s two dynamic workshops, one on rope and one on hoop. This is a fantastic opportunity to add more flow, power, and playful movement to your aerial practice.

Dynamic rope

Beats, turns, sneaky hand slides, simple releases, drops, and fast-paced combos – plus a clear breakdown of the mechanics that make dynamic movement feel effortless. Great for Improver/Intermediate students who are comfy climbing, descending, dead-hanging, and working in front balance/catchers wrap.

Dynamic hoop

Refine your beats, explore alternate grips, try dynamic switches and drops, and learn how to use momentum to turn familiar shapes into exciting entries. Ideal if you can dead hang, mount a high hoop, and have your Amazon solid.

Freya teaches across Aerial Edge’s pro programmes and tours internationally, so expect clear guidance, creativity, and plenty of confidence-boosting wins.

And possibly…Part Two?

If these workshops fill up, Freya is keen to return for Dynamics 2.0 on Saturday 24th January. aimed at Intermediate/Advanced students and anyone who comes on the 10th and wants more.

Book now and start the year feeling strong, swingy, and inspired.

## TONIGHT'S THE NIGHT

COME SEE THE SHOW

Tonight’s the night! After months of training, creativity and collaboration, our Youth Circus Performance Programme students take to the stage with Midwinter, a brand new show they’ve built from the ground up.

Our talented trio – Ivy, Sayuri and Cora – have poured everything into this piece. Join us in celebrating what these young artists have achieved.

Their last show, The Life of A Wild Thing, highlighted their strength as individual performers. For Midwinter, they set themselves the challenge of elevating their ensemble work to create something unified and bold.

With choreography support and direction from dance acro specialist Michelle Ross, and group acro coaching from Julia Chan and Robyn Gray, they’ve crafted a piece that reflects their growth as both artists and a team.

Come Support Our Young Performers

We encourage all Youth Circus students and families to come along tonight.

Tickets are free, because we want every young person to see what’s possible – and to realise that they, too, can be part of something like this.

## Showing on

A quick reminder that all Wednesday and Sunday Youth Circus students are invited to take part in our end-of-term showings tomorrow, 14th December at 1pm. Families, friends and supporters are encouraged to join us and celebrate everything our young circus stars have been building since the summer.

This is a relaxed, low-pressure event where students can choose what feels right for them. With so many skills covered across their classes, everyone will have the chance to share one or two favourites.

For anyone wanting a little extra confidence boost, we’ll have an informal practice session at 11.30am on the day.

We can’t wait to see their creativity and courage take centre stage. Come along and cheer them on!

## Roll on through til New Year

Your Youth Circus membership will roll on automatically through the two-week Christmas break. The cost of the missed classes is part of the calculation – membership is based on 48 weeks of classes in a year, divided by 12 months.

•⁠ ⁠The last class before Christmas will be Sunday 21st December, and Youth Circus will re-start in the New Year on Wednesday 7th January (11th for Sunday classes).

•⁠ ⁠At Easter, the only two dates when there will be no classes are Sunday 5th April and Wednesday 8th.

•⁠ ⁠There will be a week of camp from Monday 13th April until Friday 17th (ie classes will run on Sunday 12th and Wednesday 15th April).
