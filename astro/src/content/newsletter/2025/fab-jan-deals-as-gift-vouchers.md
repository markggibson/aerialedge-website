## SHARING THE LOVE OF CIRCUS

TIS THE SEASON TO BE JOYFUL AFTER ALL

Circus has a way of building confidence and connection at any age, which is just one reason why our January gift vouchers and membership deals feel like an interesting twist on someone's Christmas list. It’s a season for sharing experiences that bring people together, after all.

Whether you want to introduce a friend or family member to circus, or provide a hefty hint that you’d love a bit of aerial fun in your own stocking, these offers give a chance to start the year trying something new and joyful.

That sense of growth and possibility is at the heart of everything we see in Youth Circus. Between next weekend’s Performance Programme show Midwinter and the showings for all youth students, it will be wonderful to see the confidence and creativity of our young performers shining on stage.

Circus isn’t just something you do, it’s something you feel, share and become part of.

## MAKE IT A LEAP YEAR

TIME TO TURN YOUR WORLD UPSIDE DOWN

2026 may not be a leap year but it can be for you! If  you’ve ever dreamed of giving your circus journey a real leap forward, take the plunge into a full-time training programme for four weeks or four months. We tell ourselves it's not the right time, but ask yourself: when the will the right time be right now?

Whether you’re completely new to circus, already training recreationally, or considering a creative career, full-time training offers something life-changing: the time, space and expert support to truly grow.

What sets Aerial Edge apart is more than the curriculum. You train in Scotland’s biggest and longest-established circus space, surrounded by coaches who bring decades of diverse professional experience across aerial, acrobatics and dance. Our teaching ethos is deeply supportive and tailored to each student, helping you build strength, confidence and artistry in ways that work for your style of learning and moving.

The experience goes beyond technique. Students develop creative vision, storytelling skills, act creation experience and stage confidence, culminating in professionally directed shows and a unique, high-quality showreel that sets Aerial Edge apart from every other international programme. Each one has a different feel – the short clip above of Anna's showreel is an example.

Most importantly, you join a warm, welcoming community where people of all abilities are encouraged to thrive. If you want a year with challenge, transformation and joy, the right time is right now.

## COME TO THE YOUTH SHOW

MONTHS OF PREP TO MAKE MIDWINTER MAGICAL

Our Youth Circus Performance Programme students return to the stage this December with Midwinter, a brand new show created after months of training, teamwork and creative exploration.

This talented trio – Ivy, Sayuri and Cora – have poured heart, strength and imagination into bringing this production to life. Although their classmate Sukayna is unable to perform due to injury, she’ll be there in the audience cheering on these young stars with all the pride they deserve.

Their last performance, The Life of A Wild Thing in June, showcased their growth as individual artists. For Midwinter, the group set themselves a new challenge: to push their ensemble choreography to the next level.

To support this goal, they’ve been coached by dance-acro specialist Michelle Ross, who also directs the show. The teaching team included Julia Chan and Robyn Gray, whose own journey in partner acrobatics at Aerial Edge has evolved into several standout performances – perfect inspiration for the next generation.

We strongly encourage all Youth Circus students and families to come along to the short show. Tickets are free, to give every student the chance to understand that they too could do something like this.

Working towards a show twice a year builds so much more than aerial, acro and technical skill. It nurtures creative thinking, discipline, stage presence, storytelling, collaboration and confidence – qualities that grow strongest when shared with an audience.

Join us for an evening of imagination, movement and winter wonder as these young artists bring their vision to the stage.

## All in for showings

We’re excited to invite all Wednesday and Sunday Youth Circus students to take part in our end-of-term showcase on Sunday 14th December at 1pm. Families, friends and supporters are very welcome to come along and celebrate everything our young circus stars have been working on since summer.

This is a fun, low-pressure event. Students can choose to perform solo, partner up for duos, or join small groups – whatever makes them feel confident and excited to step on stage. With so many disciplines being taught through their classes each week, each performer will have the chance to share one or two of their favourite skills.

Anyone who’d like a little extra practice is welcome to come along to the 11.30am class on the day for informal rehearsals.

We can’t wait to see their creativity, courage and progress shine – come cheer them on!

## No need to rebook in January

There's no need to do anything about rebooking for Youth Circus in January. Your membership will roll on automatically after the two-week Christmas break. The cost of the missed classes is part of the calculation – membership is based on 48 weeks of classes in a year, divided by 12 months.

•⁠ ⁠The last class before Christmas will be Sunday 21st December, and Youth Circus will re-start in the New Year on Wednesday 7th January (11th for Sunday classes).

•⁠ ⁠At Easter, the only two dates when there will be no classes are Sunday 5th April and Wednesday 8th.

•⁠ ⁠There will be a week of camp from Monday 13th April until Friday 17th (ie classes will run on Sunday 12th and Wednesday 15th April).
