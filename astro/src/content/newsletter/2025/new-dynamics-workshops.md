## BE COOL AND HANG OUT AT THE BAR

LEARN, EXPLORE AND GROW WITH FREYA ON TRAPEZE

Static trapeze offers endless possibilities for strength, balance, and creative expression. Each session is a chance to explore new positions, refine technique, and discover what your body can do on the bar.

Freya brings decades of trapeze experience to the class, from her first classes as a child to teaching students of all levels. Her kind, gentle approach is paired with a quirky sense of humour, making every session approachable while still challenging you to reach your potential.

Expect clear demonstrations, thoughtful breakdowns of tricky moves, and solutions to help you progress. Freya’s classes are supportive, encouraging at all times, and full of creative inspiration. You’ll leave with new ways to move, experiment, and enjoy the art of trapeze.

Don’t forget long sleeves, leggings, and thick socks, and get ready to embrace your time on the bar.

## ONE NIGHT, SO MANY CIRCUS STORIES

COURAGE AND CREATIVITY COLLIDE AT OPEN RIG NIGHT

The line-up for Open Rig Night is locked, and it’s pure magic in the making. Behind every act is a story of courage, creativity, and the sheer joy of putting yourself out there.

Former Foundation Course students Lucy Hutton and Jamie Davidson are debuting new pieces. Lucy has been developing a straps act, blending grace with power, while Jamie combines rope and silks to illustrate the pull between the office world and the freedom of the air.

Yara, usually a flow artist who improvises performances, is stepping into something new – her first choreographed hula hoop act. Teacher Sam has a fun new act to entertain and impress you. And while Julia Chan has been seen on aerial hoop and with her acro partner Robyn at Open Rig Nights, this time she is on Chinese pole for the first time on stage.

There’s partnership from Jonah and H, showing the strength of a year’s hard work in hand-to-hand training, and aerial silks artistry from instructor Kirsty Arnott and student Eilean Denniston, who’s taking to the air for her first-ever circus performance.

Lizzie Crane brings a deeply personal touch – a hula hoop act inspired by Tamil music and culture, born from her recovery from injury. Expect the unexpected from Lilla Kovago, whose hoop act channels rebellion and dark delight.

And look out for Johnathan Cheung and Sukayna Zainabid Akber, who have both been training since they were small kids and now dream of joining a professional circus troupe. Johnathan combines acro with manipulation and silks, while Sukayna, one of the stars in our Youth Circus Performance Programme, is trialling a new kind of dance trapeze act.

From newcomers to seasoned artists, from silks to pole to dance trapeze, these acts are honest, daring, and alive.

Be there when the lights go up. One night only.

## FEEL THE FREEDOM

FIND THE ART OF DYNAMIC LETTING GO WITH FREYA

If you’ve ever wanted to really move in the air – not just hang there – Freya Pellie is here to show you how. Their one-off Dynamic Rope and Dynamic Hoop workshops will help you build the strength, timing, and flow to make the air move with you.

Freya’s approach is all about freedom through form so you are building control and you can let go with confidence. You’ll explore new beat pathways, sharper transitions, and dynamic entries that open up a whole new vocabulary of aerial movement.

Expect to leave with a toolkit of efficient, snappy techniques, and moments that make you feel like gravity’s just a suggestion.

These sessions are for aerialists with a regular practice who are ready to explore dynamics in a new way. Variations and progressions will keep everyone challenged, whether you’re just starting to find your swing or already working toward release drops.
