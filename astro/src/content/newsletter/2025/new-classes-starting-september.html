TIPPY-TOP OF YOUR TO-DO LIST

ONLY 1 SPACE LEFT IN EACH KERRI WORKSHOP

There's only one space left in each of Kerri's two workshops next Saturday, so if you're at all curious about tippy hoop or hoop-silks, get in there! We don't have another date in the diary for these arts (although if you give us feedback, you could influence which one of them would be in the October Edge).

As the clip above shows, hoops-silks isn't just a case of rigging silks to your hoop so that you can climb up to it – far from it! There are innumerable ways to combine the two arts in integrated ways.

Tipping the hoop also delivers dramatic new ways to play with hoop, especially with drops.

Don't worry if you get an imagination block, Kerri will definitely find moves that suit you!

FLIP, FLY AND WOW

NEW CIRCUS ACROBATICS CLASS FOR AGES 7-18

Take your circus skills to the next level with a totally new Youth Circus Acrobatics class with Doretto!

But be quick – there are only six spots in this class.

If you already love acro and aerial, Doretto's class is great for adding flair with flippin' heck!

He's an artistic gymnastics coach in his home country of Brazil (as well as a parkour champ and coach) so this is a fantastic chance to learn and refine handstands, cartwheels, walkovers, tumbling passes, and partner tricks, then link them into bold combos.

If you're up for it, you can learn other cool things like wall flip, palm flip, palm spin and more...Doretto is here to help you!

Challenge yourself, discover new tricks, and leave every class stronger, more confident, and ready to wow.

Strong acrobatics are also ideal for act creation. You can make jaw-dropping entrances with a ground-based acrobatic sequence leading into the aerial take-off. You can add acrobatic segments and transitions in your act that make your aerials and story-telling even more spectacular.

All of the above adds up to...Opportunity with a capital O! Take the chance while you can.
