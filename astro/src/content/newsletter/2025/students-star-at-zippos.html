LIFE IS FULL OF OPPORTUNITIES

YOUTH STUDENTS STAR AT ZIPPOS CIRCUS

There are a lot of happy people today who are enjoying the afterglow of the Youth Circus Performance Programme students pulling off a fabulous showcase at Zippos CIrcus yesterday.

We are grateful to the touring circus for inviting us to give a short performance right ahead of their main show when they were in Glasgow – it was brilliant!

Our students stepped up to the opportunity with excitement and did themselves, their families and Aerial Edge proud.

A glorious moment like that can change a person's life, giving them a sense of achievement, validation of their skills, talents and dreams, and inspiration to do more with them.

And it certainly enhances the lives of Aerial Edge staff to see the pleasure it creates.

That's one of the reasons why we do what we do. It's why we make our classes and courses as low-priced as we possibly can without going bankrupt – to give as many people as possible the joy of circus training and the advantages that come with it.

If you're able to donate a Youth Circus class, it really helps. Others who couldn't otherwise come to circus get that opportunity – and that can often open the door to more opportunities in their lives.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

BUILDING ACRO BLOCKS

NEW CLASS WORKS FOR ALL LEVELS

Ready to turn Monday blues into woohoos?! Join Group & Partner Acro, a brand new class designed to build, correct and refine techniques along with developing strength, trust and consistency.

Julia and Robyn’s mission is to advance the level of partner acro at Aerial Edge, and they believe in what you can achieve. You can be kept safe in lines so that you can try bigger, more adventurous projects.

Whatever your level is now, this class will raise your game. The teaching will be tailored to the needs of each individual – no need to come with a partner.

Expect skill-building drills with two-highs, two-high walkings, foot to hand, step ups, dynamic tempos, and more. You'll see your progress week to week.

There will also be a key focus for each class that's different each week, such as hand to hand, banquine or two-high variations.

And you'll get loads of ideas to take into your next acro jam!

STARS OF ZIPPOS SHOW

FAB OPPORTUNITY FOR YOUTH STUDENTS

Performing in a big top tent – what an unforgettable opportunity for our Youth Circus Performance Programme students!

Zippos Circus contacted us to offer the chance to perform a 15-minute showcase before their main show, and four of our amazing students were available. Sukayna, Ivy, Cora, and Sayuri leapt at the challenge.

There wasn't much time but thanks to six months of creating their June ensemble show The Life of a Wild Thing under director Michelle Ross, they were performance-ready.

After a quickly arranged one-off rehearsal session to adapt their acts, they dazzled the big top audience – a memory they’ll carry for life. Whenever the face challenges in future, they'll know that they were able to pull this off – and they loved it!

The Performance Programme (ages 10–18) offers more than regular circus classes. Alongside developing skills and techniques, PP students work as an ensemble to create a full show from scratch, gaining stage experience and the confidence that comes with it.

Classes resume on August 13, as the group begins preparing for their December show. Want to join PP? Email info@contact.aerialedge.co.uk to find out how.
