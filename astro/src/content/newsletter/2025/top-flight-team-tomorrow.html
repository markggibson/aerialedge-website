CORE SKILLS WITH TRUST

TEAMWORK, STRENGTH & SERIOUS BRAIN TRAINING

We're always talking about being in the air, but sometimes the coolest moves happen right on the mat. Our Youth Circus students are absolutely crushing the acrobalance game, and it’s about way more than just flexibility – it’s a masterclass in trust and physics.

The counterweight is a core burner. It starts with the base lying down, holding their partner’s legs while the flyer holds a plank. Then, in perfect sync, they execute a massive sit-up into that seriously stylish box shape you see – super focused, super strong!

And the people pyramid is the definition of team energy. Every student is a critical piece, from the solid bases to the smiling flyer up top.

Why do we love love acrobalance as much as students? Because it's a direct route to building proprioception – your body's hidden sense of where its parts are in space. When you're constantly balancing, shifting weight, and coordinating with a partner, you train your brain to have an insanely accurate awareness of your body.

This isn't just useful for a perfect handstand; it translates directly to confidence, coordination, and physical intelligence in everything else they do, from sports to catching a bus. They're basically training to be super-aware humans.

Awesome work! Keep building that strength and trust.
