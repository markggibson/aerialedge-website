Winter is upon us, so please be prepared for Youth Circus classes with layers of clothing on both tops and bottoms – and definitely don't leave home without warm socks! It's way better to have too many layers than too few.

While we have a couple of heaters, and we do warm-up exercises, it's cold in the space – so save the shorts, T-shirts and bare feet until spring. Please come with leggings, joggers, and long-sleeved fleeces, jumpers or hoodies. It's not just for comfort, it's also for safety because cold muscles and ligaments are more susceptible to injuries.

Holiday dates

  • The last class before Christmas will be Sunday 21st December, and Youth Circus will re-start in the New Year on Wednesday 7th January (11th for Sunday classes).

  • At Easter, the only two dates when there will be no classes are Sunday 5th April or Wednesday 8th.

  • There will be a week of camp from Monday 13th April until Friday 17th (ie classes will run on Sunday 12th and Wednesday 15th April.
