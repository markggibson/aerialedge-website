## CHANGE IS BETTER THAN REST

NEW CLASSES TO KEEP IT FRESH FOR YOU

Circus is all about transformation – turning fear into flight, hesitation into trust, effort into artistry.

That spirit of change is at the core of everything we do, not just for variety, but because you are evolving too. Your curiosity inspires us to keep things fresh, challenging, and full of possibility.

This August, you can try four new classes designed to support your growth, wherever you are on your circus journey, and of course our new October edition of the popular Easter Edge aerial and acro festival.

Keep exploring. We’ll keep creating for you.

Mark Gibson

## NEW ACROBALANCE CLASS

TAKE YOUR FUN TO A NEW LEVEL

Get ready to lift, balance, and connect...our new acrobalance and partner acro class starts in August!

Julia and Robyn discovered and developed their passion for this art while training in our full-time courses, and this class is your chance to learn from performers who’ve turned partnership into a powerful creative practice.

You’ve probably already tried a few moves for fun – we see people doing that all the time!

Now you can dive in properly, build technique, and explore what’s possible when trust and movement come together. Whether you're new or experienced, you're going to love this.

Acrobalance & Partner Acro

Monday 4th August | 6.30pm-8pm

Cost: £20 drop-in or use your class pack

## HAMMOCK IS IN OCTOBER EDGE

VERSATILE ART FOR YOU TO LOVE

For the first time ever, hammock is coming to our Edge festival line-up – and tickets are NOW available to snap up!

Following feedback from Kerri's recent hammock workshop which filled up fast, she’s bringing her dynamic, creative teaching style to the October Edge, the new autumn edition of our popular Easter Edge aerial and acro festival.

Hammock is endlessly versatile – from elegant still poses to powerful drops and dynamic sequences – and Kerri will guide you through it all, whether you’re just starting out or ready to level up.

Alongside hammock, you’ll find your favourite disciplines at the October Edge: flying trapeze, silks, rope, straps, acro and more.

The October Edge
