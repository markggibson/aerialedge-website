Hi Mark,

As you may know, there is a Rangers-Celtic match at Ibrox this Sunday, with a kick-off at 12noon.

Please allow plenty of time to get to classes as there will be slow-moving traffic queues.

Police Scotland advice

Police Scotland have told us that you will be able to drive from the south down Broomloan Road into the car park except for a 15-minute period around 2pm. When you exit, you will have to turn left to keep the traffic flowing in that direction.

Alternatively, Police Scotland suggested parking at Starbucks/Aldi and walking along Edmiston Drive to the school.

They have no plans to shut Edmiston Road between the roundabout at Helen Street (Starbucks) and the one at Broomloan Road (outside our building) and they have strategies in place to keep the opposing fans apart to prevent hostilities. Edmiston Road will be shut in front of Ibrox stadium, possibly as early as 9.30am. They expect the traffic to be clear by around 3pm.

As with all big football matches, the situation is fluid and in the case of conflict arising, other roads may be shut.

Celtic fan buses will be in Govan Road and fans on public transport will  come via Govan Subway station – they have been asked to be there by 9.30am. Rangers fan buses will be in Helen Street, and fans on Subway will come from Ibrox station. They will be admitted to the stadium around 10.30am.

Our advice

Because of slow-moving queues, we would advise you to wait in reception during the class rather than dropping your child and returning to pick them up – we have tea, coffee and internet to sustain you!

See you soon,

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge, Glasgow's Circus School
