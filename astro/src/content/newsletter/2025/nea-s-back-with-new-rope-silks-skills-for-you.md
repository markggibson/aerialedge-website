## MAKE THE MOST OF THE FRINGE

YOUR BEST CHANCE FOR INSPIRATION

Hopefully you're taking some time to catch some shows at the Edinburgh Fringe – we're so lucky to have some of the best circus performers in the world on our doorstep for these few weeks.

As well as being a great day and/or night out, it's fantastic inspiration. It makes you think about your own practice and consider how aerial and acrobatic skills can become performance when you add elements such as surprise, theatrical skills, storytelling and sometimes costume.

Technical skills are one thing, performance is a whole different set of abilities that can be learned by anyone who has the inclination. We'd love you to consider preparing an act for the next Open Rig Night, and for Youth Circus students, think about the Performance Programme.

You won't know you love it until you try it...and then there's no looking back.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

## NEA HERE AGAIN

TEACHING HER NEW SKILLS AT OCTOBER EDGE

Nea is back from Barcelona next week and all set to bring her newly acquired knowledge to your classes – and the October Edge.

After training rope and silks up to seven hours a day at La Bonita, she’s brimming with new techniques and new ways to explain existing ones.

She says: "I have expanded my knowledge on dynamics. On silks, people can definitely expect even more confusing sequences!"

As well as rope and silks, the October Edge gives you the chance to try new arts and make great progress in your favourites – set your goals with your instructor and go for it over the three days. You'll do workshops in the same four disciplines every day so you can really focus.
