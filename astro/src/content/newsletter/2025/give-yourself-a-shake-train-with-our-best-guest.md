## WHY GUESTS ARE GOOD FOR YOU

GIVE YOURSELF A SHAKE

One of the best things about running Aerial Edge is seeing how the right teacher at the right time can transform our training. We have some of the best instructors you could hope to find right here at Aerial Edge, nonetheless bringing in guests is important to inspire and shake us up a bit.

So I’m pretty stoked about Jason Brügger coming to teach at the October Edge – I for one will be doing his workshops, and I’d encourage you to do the same, whether or not straps is currently your thing.

Jason isn’t just an exceptional aerialist, he’s an artist who has built his career by constantly pushing boundaries, blending grace, strength, and storytelling in ways that inspire audiences internationally.

Training with someone new of this calibre gives us a chance to experience what’s possible for ourselves when artistry and athleticism come together at the highest level. I’m sure it’s going to spark new ideas and new possibilities for everyone who takes part.

Expansion like this is what we aim to create through The October Edge: days filled with discovery, and the kind of breakthroughs in self-development that perhaps only can happen when you step outside your normal routine and dive in fully.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

## LIVING THE HIGH LIFE

JULIA & ROBYN LEAD WITH FUN AT OCTOBER EDGE

If you’ve been to an Aerial Edge show, you already know how Julia & Robyn light up the stage with their partner acro – and now you get the chance to train with them in depth at October Edge.

The three-day aerial and acro festival is enough time to nail down a tricky technique, or throw yourself into your first adventure of hand-to-hand or two-highs. Expect all kinds of other highs as your skills go up. Laughter will echo long after class ends because these two lead with fun.

They first teamed up on our Foundation Course, both coming from a strong gymnastics background. Years of full-time training later, they’ve developed a partnership that’s become a crowd favourite. Dynamic, daring, endearing and often light-hearted, their acro always gets people smiling.

Off stage, they’re the glue of our acro community – running the monthly Acro Jam, and bringing their unique brand of fun to our Monday acro classes. In both cases, the goals are stacking humans into improbable shapes and getting higher, faster and more creative with the moves.

If you’ve ever looked around at people balancing on each other, this is your moment. Book in, and let Julia & Robyn show you just how high we can go together.

## MAKE THIS YOUR MOMENT

IT'S NOW OR NEVER (OR AT LEAST NOT UNTIL SPRING)

The clock’s ticking…if you’ve been tempted by taking a step forward in circus training, now’s the time. Our Four-Week Circus Intensive starts 29th September, and it’s your last chance to train like this until next March.

Don’t let another season pass you by. Step off the sidelines and into the ring!

Why do this?

This intensive is designed for you, no matter what level you’re at. No prior circus experience is required, and if you’ve already got skills, we adapt each lesson to each individual. Guaranteed, no one is good at everything! We're experts at pitching the teaching the right way for your learning type and level.

Just bring your enthusiasm and a willingness to learn. In the four weeks, you will:

-

• Broaden your skillset across multiple circus disciplines

-

• Experience full-time training without a long-term commitment

-

• Create and perform a solo act in a supportive environment

-

• Test the waters before committing to our full-time Foundation Course

What you’ll learn

Aerial Edge is unusual in offering the most popular art of all, flying trapeze. Over the four weeks, you’ll all train in a broad range of other arts such as:

-

• Aerial: Static trapeze, rope, silks, hoop and straps

-

• Acrobatics: Handstands, dance acro, trampoline, teeterboard

-

• Flow Arts: Hula hoop, rope dart, juggling

-

• Foundational skills: Flexibility, conditioning, safe training techniques

-

• Performance Skills: Act creation, circus performance practice, rigging & safety

There's also scope for learning other skills such as stilt walking, tightwire etc. Each day is packed with structured sessions, culminating in a performance opportunity to showcase your newly acquired skills.

A proven pathway

Many students use this intensive as a stepping stone to our four-month Foundation Course, which delves deeper into technique, performance, and act creation, culminating in an ensemble show with a professional director. Some people even return for the next intensive to further refine their skills.

## LET YOUR LIGHT SHINE

YOU CAN DO IT, WE KNOW YOU CAN

If you haven't performed before at an Open Rig Night, WHY NOT?! Seriously. Ask yourself for real. What is it that makes you refuse to consider it? What are the voices in your head saying to make you think you can't do it?

Free yourself from your fear, as Marianne Williamson says above. And as Mark has said in previous newsletters, we've had people who refused to perform, but we've never had someone who gathered their courage, took the step and regretted it.

You can start with these small steps:

1.	Ask your instructor to help you string together a sequence of moves (maybe you can already do that)

2.	Choose some music that you love

3.	Practice

Chat to your instructor or one of the team about it. Don't be shy – there's nothing to be gained from that, and so much to be gained from overcoming it.
