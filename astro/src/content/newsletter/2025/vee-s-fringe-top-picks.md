## THE BUZZ OF BEGINNINGS

BRIGHT NEW THINGS ARE STARTING

If you're quick and decisive, you have just enough time to join the next Foundation or Circus Intensive Course which both start on Monday.

There's something extra special about these, because it's the start of the educational year, and they begin together, so students from both courses train together for the first month.

That's when there's a broad range of arts being taught, and everyone's got different abilities in each art – no one is good at everything. It's a great leveller which naturally leads to cohesive groups with mutual support and encouragement.

People's strengths – not just in circus – come into play and new ones emerge.

It's the same newness buzz as Youth Circus begins again after the summer break. Friendships begin or are revived, along with the spirit of adventure and exploration.

Let the playing commence!

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

## READY FOR PERFORMANCE?

CHOOSE WHICH CLASS TO TAKE

Finally! The summer hols are over and Youth Circus is back!

Now is a good time to decide if you want to switch from regular classes to the Performance Programme – you could be like Ivy, Sukayna, Cora and Sayuri performing in a real big top last week for a big audience when Zippos Circus was in town!

The four were able to jump at the chance because they were stage-ready at the drop of a hat. That's because they'd already created fully fledged acts during the term leading up to the Performance Programme's last show, The Life of a Wild Thing.

Performance Programme is open to ages 10-18 for students who enjoy a slightly more structured learning framework than the regular classes. It's all still playful and fun exploring aerial and acrobatic techniques and tricks.

It's just that there's a show to create for December, which provides natural motivation to progress as the students collaborate to turn their ideas into their ensemble performance.
