## SPIN AND FLY ON POLE

NEW WORKSHOPS FOR ALL LEVELS WITH ELKE

Take pole work off the floor with Elke Bucher in two fly pole workshops that’ll get you entering, climbing, spinning, and holding tricks whether you are beginner or advanced level.

The workshops focus on technique, safety, and the unique movement challenges of a rubber-coated, free-spinning pole. You’ll learn entry methods. There will be anti-dizziness strategies inspired by ballet and aerial. And you'll build combos adapted to your personal skill level.

Elke isn’t about the effortless performance look – they’re all about curiosity, finding control, and pushing yourself in the air.

A dedicated aerialist with a long-standing love for pole, hoop, and hair hanging, they’ve performed across the UK and abroad from Steel Panther in Germany to London’s House of Serpents. And they've placed in Miss Pole Dance UK and multiple Pole Sports Org competitions too.

Beginners: no experience required, just a bit of fitness and an adventurous spirit.

Intermediate/Advanced: some experience with shiny pole or Chinese pole and comfort with inverts will let you dive deeper.

Expect strong, ethereal shapes, tricky spins, and a chance to really get to know the unique flow of a fly pole.

## Diehard dynamics

Hanging around isn't enough for some people. Freya's dynamic workshops will get you riding the beats, hitting crisp transitions, and finding that moment when gravity stops mattering.

You’ll build the strength and timing to make your apparatus come alive – learning how to catch the moment, let go, and trust the swing.

Think beats that flow, transitions that pop, and those mid-air moments that genuinely feel like magic.

Variations keep it spicy, so wherever you are – beginner beatnik or release-drop chaser – you’ll find your style. If you’ve got a regular practice and you’re ready to push your dynamics, this is your playground.
