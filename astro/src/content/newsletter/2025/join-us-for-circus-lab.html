HEAD DOWN FOR CHINESE POLE

DIVE IN AT THE OCTOBER EDGE

The October Edge is your chance to dive headfirst into Chinese pole – or more accurately, down the Chinese pole. Over the three days, Edd will give you the climbs, the grips, and the confidence o unlock this dynamic discipline in one intense, satisfying hit.

With Edd's pole classes also back on the weekly schedule, you have the chance to build up to the October Edge, and keep building beyond it.

This October Edge track is suitable for you if:

💥 You’re a seasoned pole person who wants to develop ballistic style

💥 You’re a shiny pole person who wants to transfer skills

💥 You’re a beginner who wants to learn a cool trick or two

One of the best things about this track is how quickly you’ll find yourself doing moves that look dramatic but are surprisingly accessible. Sliding down headfirst? That can actually be your day one Instagram moment.

But Edd knows that real progress comes from mastering the climbs. Across the three progressive October Edge workshops, you’ll get those climbs nailed, giving you the strength and technique to build into the ballistic fun.

Edd’s own training followed the traditional Chinese style – sudden bursts of energy, flipping from static holds to somersaults and catches. Moves like fish-to-flag, Superman swings, or the iconic human flag are all part of this acrobatic path.

You can also work on the ever-popular fongi, a shoulder-mount swing with a twist that feels as good as it looks.

Chinese pole is different from shiny pole: the rubber covering gives grip instead of slide, so you can throw yourself into dynamic moves without losing purchase. What you’ll need is body tension and strength to hold positions – the reward is a style that looks explosive, impressive, and unapologetically circus.

What to bring: soft, flat rubber-soled shoes – eg Feiyue or plimsolls, but not trainers. Plenty of layers – leggings, jeans, long sleeves, sweatshirt.

FOX'S GREMLIN PLAYGROUND

WEIRD WRAPS & BOLD DROPS FOR ROPE EXPLORERS

Join Fox McLavin: aerialist, rope specialist, and self-proclaimed aerial gremlin. Their style is shaped not only by nearly a decade in the air, but also by lived experience as a queer, neurodivergent artist with a very real fear of heights. The result? An inventive, playful approach to rope that turns problem-solving into truly unique movement.

This one-off workshop is for folk who want to explore fluidity and creature-like qualities in their aerial practice—but it’s also really about finding movements on rope that feel good in your body, and letting that sensation lead the way. If twisting is your jam, Fox has sequences designed to help you dive right into that spiral world. Alongside, you’ll experiment with creative wraps, drops that tumble into new perspectives, rope-as-frame play, and lassos with a twist.

Designed for intermediate to advanced rope practitioners, Gremlin 101 is an invitation to get weird, follow your instincts, and discover how good “gremlin movement” can feel.

Prerequisites: hiplock from a scissor kick, solid ankle wraps, flamenco grips, and catchers.

CIRCUS RAW AND REAL

WORKS IN PROGRESS, DARING EXPERIMENTS, FRESH IDEAS

Think of Open Rig Night as a circus laboratory, where artists bring their experiments to life. It will feature finished polished performances, and also some circus in its raw, developing stages.

But this isn't improvised chaos. It can be short acts, works-in-progress, and new creations, all there to be tested in front of a live audience.

This is a chance to witness the sparks of ideas – acts that might one day become polished showstoppers, or pieces that thrive in their rough, adventurous form. It’s immediate, creative, and exciting.

For performers, Open Rig is your opportunity to put new work in front of people without the pressure of perfection. Whether you’re building an act for the first time or taking a familiar idea in a bold new direction, you’ll have the space to experiment and the support of a generous audience.

It’s circus stripped back to its most essential elements: risk, play, and discovery. Come join us in the lab.
