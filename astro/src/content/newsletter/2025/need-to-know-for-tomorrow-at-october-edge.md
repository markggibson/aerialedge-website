Get ready for a great weekend!

Hey Mark,

Look lively lovely circus folk, here's what you need to know for the October Edge tomorrow:

Warm up

We'll open up at 9.15am so that you can get caffeinated and get changed before we start the group warm-up around 9.30am. The bean-to-cup machine will be prepped, it's used to dealing with a demanding daily delivery!

Dress up

Speaking of getting changed, we encourage you to dress in whatever Halloween costume you fancy and/or get creative with spookily themed or horror make-up. Just make sure that your costume...

-

DOESN'T have trailing ends that could trap you on the apparatus

-

DOESN'T have hard or sharp accessories that could damage apparatus (you'd be surprised how one small ring can wreak havoc)

-

DOES have long sleeves and legs to protect you not just from friction burns but from the cold. The space is likely to be chilly but you'll be building up some internal heat, so think layers.

-

DOES include layers top and bottom if you're doing Chinese pole (ie long-sleeve T shirt and sweatshirt plus jeans with leggings underneath). You'll also want rubber-soled shoes like Feiyue or plimsolls, trainers don't work so well.

Share the fun

Share your Instagram moments @aerial_edge – we'll give away a free class at the end of the three days for the post that makes us smile the most. Plus, as we are a non-profit and make circus as accessible as possible, you'll be helping our mission to bring circus to as many people as possible.

Fabulous finish

After a satisfying day's training, what we need is a satisfying meal. We're planning to head to Beirut Star, a good Lebanese restaurant near the school. It doesn't have an alcohol licence but you can bring a bottle and there's a licensed Co-Op next to it.

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact our team at 07977 068511 or info@aerialedge.co.uk.

See you soon,

Mark Gibson
