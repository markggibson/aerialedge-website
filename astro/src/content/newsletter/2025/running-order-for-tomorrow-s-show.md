Here's a quick reminder of the day's schedule...

Rehearsals – from 4:00pm

The space will be open from 4pm if you’d like to rehearse your act or get comfortable with the rig.

Tech Run – 6:00pm (Call Time)

Please be ready by 6pm, with costume and make-up done, and all your equipment ready.
We’ll walk through the running order to confirm rigging changes and cues – this isn’t a full act rehearsal, but it’s important for giving everyone the best chance of it running smoothly.

Doors Open – 7:15pm

From 7:15pm, we'll start seating guests so you can stay off-stage in the staff room, or interact with guests as they arrive.

Gabby's been working hard to set up everyone’s  cues and lighting requests and we're excited that this is looking like a great night. – though with so many acts and quick changes, there may be a few small variations on the night. Thank you in advance for rolling with any small surprises on the night, live performances certainly keep you light on your feet!

If you have any questions or need help with tech, costume, or timing, just let Gabby or Mark know, or email info@contact.aerialedge.co.uk

See you at the stage,

Mark
