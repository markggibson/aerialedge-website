Hi Mark,

It’s almost time for you to step into the spotlight! Thank you for taking part, it’s shaping up to be our best Open Rig Night yet, and we’re here to help make it a great experience for everyone from first time performers to seasoned stage stars.

We’ll share the final running order soon. For now, here’s everything you need to know about the day:

Rehearsals – from 4:00pm

The space will be open from 4pm if you’d like to rehearse your act or get comfortable with the rig.

Tech Run – 6:00pm (Call Time)

Please be ready by 6pm, with costume and make-up done, and all your equipment ready.
We’ll walk through the running order to confirm rigging changes and cues – this isn’t a full act rehearsal, but it’s important for giving everyone the best chance of it running smoothly.

Doors Open – 7:15pm

From 7:15pm, please stay off-stage in the staff room so we can seat the audience.
If you’d like to interact with guests as they arrive, feel free – it always adds to the fun atmosphere, but it’s completely optional.

 

We can’t wait to see all your creativity coming together into one magical night. You’ll have plenty of support from your fellow artists, and the team will do everything possible to help. We’ll do our utmost to get everyone’s exact cues and lighting specifications right – though with so many acts and quick changes, there may be a few small variations on the night. Thank you in advance for being flexible; it all adds to the spirit of live performance!

If you have any questions or need help with tech, costume, or timing, just let Gabby or Mark know, or email info@contact.aerialedge.co.uk

See you soon!
