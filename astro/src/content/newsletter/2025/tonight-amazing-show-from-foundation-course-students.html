DITCH EFFORT, GO FLOW

MASTERING MOMENTUM MADE EASY

Here’s why you need to prioritise Freya’s dynamic rope and hoop workshops (which have been rescheduled to Saturday 10th January).

This isn't just about swinging and beating, this is about fundamentally changing how you relate to your apparatus.

Most aerial artists rely on sheer strength, which can lead to fatigue, frustration, and eventual burnout. Freya’s method flips that script entirely.

The Freya Difference: science, not struggle

Freya specialises in teaching the biomechanics of momentum. She taps into principles of physics and body efficiency, helping you stop working against gravity and start working with it.

• Unlock flow state: Imagine movements that feel truly effortless. Freya helps you find the rhythm and timing that eliminate drag and friction, allowing you to enter the coveted "flow state" where performance feels seamless.

• Injury prevention: By replacing brute force with controlled momentum, you drastically reduce the strain on your joints and tendons, ensuring a more joy for longer.

• The click moment: Students consistently report abreakthrough – the "ohhh, that's how it works" moment – where difficult transitions suddenly become simple, freeing up energy for creativity.

Your transformation starts here

These workshops are specifically designed to meet you where you are. Whether you've avoided dynamic movements for years or are ready to refine your current technique, Freya provides:

1. Gentle Beats: Learn how to initiate momentum without power.

2. Controlled Timing: Master the exact moment to transition for maximum efficiency.

3. Creative Transitions: Elevate your routine with fluid, beautiful movements that look complex but feel simple.

Forget "too fast" or "too hard." These workshops are about learning to move smarter, not harder.

COME CHEER YOUTH PERFORMERS

EVERYONE CAN GET IN ON THE ACT

Youth Circus students from Wednesday and Sunday classes are invited to star in a showing on Sunday 14th December at 1pm, and all family and friends are invited to come along to cheer them on.

It's very relaxed, no pressure, just a chance for every student who wants to join in to show a few of the skills and talents that they have been developing since summer.
