Now you pay monthly instead of term by term

Hi Mark,

Just a reminder that Youth Circus Wednesday and Sunday classes begin this week, and we are switching to a monthly membership rather than term-by-term payments.

This means that you spread the cost throughout the year, by paying on the same date each month, rather than paying for the term up-front (step-by step instructions below).

The first payment will come out on the date of your first class, and on the same day each month thereafter.

The monthly cost of £48 has been calculated based on 90-minute classes at £12 each, running 48 of the 52 weeks in the year. That's a big discount on the drop-in price of £20 per class.

And yes – next year Youth Circus will run all through the summer holidays! The four sessions off per year will be at Christmas, Easter and October.

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to our team at 07977 068511 or info@aerialedge.co.uk.

See you soon,

How to set up your monthly membership

You can view a short video showing the instructions here.

  1. Go to the Aerial Edge website and click the 'Book A Class' button at the top

  2. Log into your TeamUp account

  3. Go to schedule at the very top of the page

  4. Click the top of the calendar until you are in August

  5. Go to the date of the first class you want to attend. Wednesdays (ages 7-18) and Performance Programme (ages 10-18) start on 13 August; Sundays (ages 7-13 and 13-18) start on 17 August; and Little Circus Stars (ages 1-6) start on Monday 18 August

  6. Click on the class you want to attend

  7. Click 'View registration options'

  8. Click the Monthly Membership option

  9. On the membership page, put your card details in, and click 'Purchase'

  10. You will see on the schedule that you are registered for that class, and you will receive a confirmation email

You can easily cancel your membership at any time:

  1. Log in to the web version of your TeamUp account. This can be done via a laptop/iPad/desktop or mobile browser.

  2. Select My Account

  3. Select Details by the membership you'd like to cancel

  4. Cancel Membership
