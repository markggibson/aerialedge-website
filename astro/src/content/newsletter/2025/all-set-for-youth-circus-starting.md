Now you pay monthly instead of term by term

Hi Mark,

Just a reminder that Youth Circus Wednesday and Sunday classes begin this week, and we are switching to a monthly membership rather than term-by-term payments.

This means that you spread the cost throughout the year, by paying on the same date each month, rather than paying for the term up-front (step-by step instructions below).

The first payment will come out on the date of your first class, and on the same day each month thereafter.

The monthly cost of £48 has been calculated based on 90-minute classes at £12 each, running 48 of the 52 weeks in the year. That's a big discount on the drop-in price of £20 per class.

And yes – next year Youth Circus will run all through the summer holidays! The four sessions off per year will be at Christmas, Easter and October.

If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to our team at 07977 068511 or info@aerialedge.co.uk.

See you soon,

Mark Gibson

Founder

Aerial Edge
