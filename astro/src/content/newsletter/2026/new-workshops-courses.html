KNOTS, LOADS, AND PEACE OF MIND

MASTER THE MECHANICS BEHIND THE MAGIC

Behind every breathtaking drop and effortless spin is a system of steel, fabric, and physics keeping you safe. While the audience sees the art, the secret of circus is the rigging – the silent, sturdy partner that holds it all together.

Our rigging courses take the guesswork out of the act. No more “fingers crossed” or “Ah, it looks ok”. Our two two-day intensive blocks get you straight on the "how" and "why" of circus safety.

There are particular differences to circus rigging as opposed to all the other types. Mainly down to the complications of having a living, beating, spinning and daringly moving person who’s on the apparatus.

These factors place entirely different forces and pressure on the equipment, which everyone who rigs for aerial acts needs to know.

There’s also a three-hour one-off workshop for instructors, performers and people who want to have apparatus at home – see the workshops section below for that.

Rigging for Circus Arts – Part 1

Think of this as your essential toolkit. We’ll cover the fundamentals: equipment types, identifying safe working loads, understanding force calculations (yes, even the maths is fun here), and the vital paperwork that makes a professional rig.

It’s about building the confidence to look at a point and know exactly what’s happening above your head.

Rigging for Circus Arts – Part 2

Ready to go higher? Part two takes those foundations and climbs further into complex systems, rescue plans, and advanced configurations.

The best part? You can choose your own adventure. If you just want to solidify your basic knowledge and feel confident about safety, jump into Part 1. Book both and become the person everyone turns to when the "how do we hang this?" questions start.

Mark brings decades of global industry experience to his teaching, ensuring your head doesn't get scrambled by the theory. It’s clear, practical, and – in true Aerial Edge style – a celebration of the craft that keeps us all flying.

THE HALF-TERM TAKEOVER

THREE DAYS OF HIGH-ALTITUDE PLAY

When school breaks for hols, energy levels usually hit the ceiling – we’re here to make good use of it!

Our three-day half-term camp is a 10am to 4pm dive into everything that makes circus the ultimate playful activity. While everyone’s blithely enjoying the aerial, acrobatic and other arts, they are unwittingly getting better at all kinds of good things like concentration, coordination, collaboration, problem solving and creative expression.

And those are boosts for all the selfs – esteem, worth, belief and more.

The undisputed king of the camp is the Flying Trapeze. Because this doesn't make an appearance in our regular weekly Youth Circus classes, everyone waits for it at camp. (Pro-tip: if they fall in love with the air, our dedicated Youth Fly class every Monday is the place to be).

Back on the ground – or slightly above it – the days are packed with aerial silks, hoop, and the rhythmic focus of object manipulation. And some real magic happens in the group acrobalance sessions. There’s a specific kind of pride that comes from being the base of a human pyramid or the star at the top, learning that support is both a physical and a social skill.

It’s three days of games, grit, and gravity-defying wins. No desks, no screens – it’s all about the thrill of the rig.
