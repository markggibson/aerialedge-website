ELEVATE YOUR RIGGING

MASTER CIRCUS-SPECIFIC AND GENERAL SKILLS

Our two-part course offers a deep dive into the specialised world of circus and entertainment rigging. Whether you are an industry professional looking to expand your technical repertoire or an aerialist wanting to take full command of your environment, this training bridges the gap between standard work-at-height and the dynamic demands of the stage.

You will get hands-on experience with the sophisticated systems that make breathtaking moments possible. Here are three highlights of the curriculum:

  • Performer flying systems – understand the mechanics of manual and mechanical hauling to create seamless, graceful movement in the air.

  • Rescue techniques – gain the high-pressure skills needed to execute safe, efficient rescues using dedicated rope systems.

  • Industry-standard precision – learn to apply rigorous safety protocols to the high-dynamic loads found only in circus arts.

You can gain the technical mastery to turn a creative vision into a safe, spectacular reality.

BURST OF ADVENTURE

3 REASONS TO CHOOSE CIRCUS FOR YOUR HOLS

Fancy an adventure holiday that’s secretly safe yet challenging? Stepping into a full-time course feels less like a training camp and more like a total departure from your everyday reality. And you can do it for one to four weeks at a time.

Here are three reasons our short intensives capture a very special holiday magic:

  • Living in the moment: when you are balanced on a bar or suspended from a silk, the emails and life admin simply cease to exist.

  • A different kind of ache: there is a deep, satisfying tiredness that comes from movement rather than the stress of being in your head all day, making your evening rest feel truly earned.

  • Finding connection: you spend your days surrounded by people who share your adventures, creating friendships that come easy with so much in common.

It is the rare chance to run away to the circus without actually having to leave your life behind. Yet you’ll return to your day-to-day life as a changed person, with new perspectives about your capabilities.

TIPS TO HELP YOU TAKE THE LEAP

YOU CAN DO IT, WE KNOW YOU CAN

Moving from a regular class to the Open Rig Night stage is a leap, and it is completely natural to wonder if you are ready. But it's really this simple: if you have a sequence you love, you are ready to show it.

This night is specifically designed as a welcoming space. If you have been on the fence about signing up, remember that the goal isn't perfection, it’s about the thrill of the try.

The most memorable performances aren't always the most technical ones, they are the ones where the performer is clearly having the time of their life.

The transition from student to performer can be just a matter of perspective. You have skills from your training, and the open rig is simply a way to share them.

Here’s some tips to help you take that step up to the stage:

  • Trust your muscle memory – your body knows these moves better than your nervous brain does. Rehearse your act, then let your training take over once the music starts.

  • Focus on the feeling – instead of worrying about every technical detail, focus on the joy of being in the air, because that’s what will shine through to the audience.

  • Embrace the butterflies – that nervous energy is actually a powerful tool that can help you stay sharp and present during your set.

  • Lean on the community – you aren't out there alone, you are surrounded by coaches and fellow students who are all rooting for your success.

Your instructor will give you all the support you need to pull this off. Ask them for help with your act, and take a step towards a moment you'll never forget. Please register using the form below by Friday 17th April.
