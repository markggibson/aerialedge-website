Hi Mark,

As you may know, there is a Rangers-Celtic match at Ibrox this Sunday, with a kick-off at 12noon. Last time this happened, access to Youth Circus classes was relatively smooth, however please allow plenty of time to get to classes as the number of fans means there will be traffic queues.

Police Scotland advice

We've been unable to reach Police Scotland, but their suggestion last time there was an Old Firm match was to park at Starbucks/Aldi and walk along Edmiston Drive to Aerial Edge, because you won't be able to stop on the road to drop people off. The car park entrance will not be open, and police will not allow you to wait outside it for us to open it for you, because they will need to keep traffic flowing.

The section of Edmiston Drive in front of Ibrox stadium (after the roundabout with Broomloan Road) may be closed, as early as 9.30am. Police have strategies in place to keep fans apart, but as with all big football matches, the situation is fluid and in the case of conflict arising, other roads may be shut at short notice.

Traffic flowed last time one way along Broomloan Road - from the motorway towards the city centre - and is likely to be the same again this time.

Roads should be back to normal by around 3pm.

Our advice

Because of traffic queues, we would advise you to wait in reception during the class rather than dropping your child and returning to pick them up – we have tea, coffee and internet to sustain you!

See you soon,

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge, Glasgow's Circus School
