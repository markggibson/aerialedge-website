## THE EDGE IS WHERE TO BE

GROWTH HAPPENS WHEN YOU LET GO

Standing in the middle of Easter Edge right now, we are enjoying the sounds, sights and smiles of people who are choosing to spend the holiday weekend outside their comfort zones.

Both this and the October Edge are special events, I love that they are progress-driven, as opposed to being a series of taster sessions. The energy is different as people are playing with their favourite arts and bravely getting to grips with new ones, in a way that blends the fun with focus. The high spirits in the room support the idea that this is a positive evolution of festival design.

Just as much consideration has gone into the concept of our new Aerial Edge Circus Challenge. We're anticipating this becoming a core element of our calendar twice or more a year, so that you can compare your results from one to the next. You're going to love the experience and the outcomes.

The only problem we haven’t quite solved is how to keep our teachers on their stations! They are so keen to jump in and take on the challenge themselves that we might have to physically restrain them from competing so they can actually supervise the scoring. When the coaches envy the students, you know it’s going to be a good day.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

## WHAT'S YOUR PERSONAL BEST?

FIND OUT AT THE AERIAL EDGE CIRCUS CHALLENGE

We’re launching a high-buzz new way to celebrate your circus skills, whether you’re at a beginner or an advanced level – the Aerial Edge Circus Challenge.

And since it’s a fantastic way to revel in the love of circus, the first edition is happening on World Circus Day, Saturday 18th April.

The Aerial Edge Circus Challenge is designed for you to gain points by completing skills across a range of different circus disciplines. It doesn’t matter whether you train one circus art of several, there are ways for you to tailor this to your level of skills.

There’s a choice of five skills for each discipline, and three levels of difficulty within each of the five.  Each level of difficulty yields a different score.

And that’s just the base of the challenge. You’ll choose what to attempt and in which order within a 100-minute window. There’s a limited number of spots available but the school will still be busy, so you’ll need to be agile in your planning in real-time to grab your chance to complete the skills you want.

Just for larks, a leaderboard will exist, but this isn’t a championship. The spirit of the event is self-comparison, growth, and community. With dozens of individual challenges on the table, there is more to do than anyone can realistically finish, so you will need to play to your strengths and choose your battles wisely.

This is a wild ride involving personal performance and exploration for all levels of experience, strategic decision-making under time pressure, and long-term progress tracking across future editions.

Here’s how it’s going to be a blast:

1. Live strategy

For excitement and equal conditions, skills remain secret until the event begins. They will be revealed via a private Instagram account. Each one will have video demos and technical rules.

2. Progressive scoring

In each discipline, there are five skills which you can attempt. Each skill (for example footlock in silks) has three levels of difficulty worth 1, 2, or 3 points.

3. Real-time validation

To bank your points, simply film your attempt and tag us in your Instagram Story. Our team will review all the Stories and log points.

4. Long-term tracking

We are creating custom visualisations of your success rate so you can compare your benchmarks from one edition to the next.

Whether you are the minimum age of 13 or the maximum of 100, the spirit of the day is self-comparison and community. Every participant will walk away with a limited-edition sticker and evidence of what their body and mind can achieve on the day of an Aerial Edge Circus Challenge.

## GIVE THEM A BOOST

WHY CIRCUS ENHANCES EDUCATION

Educators increasingly highlight the power of physical literacy – the unique combination of confidence, coordination, and the motivation to stay active for life. We deliver this through the inherent playfulness of circus training, and the non-competitive, supportive environment that we foster.

Weekly classes great, and there is even more transformative power in an immersive format. Spending a full day – or better yet, a full week – in our school allows for a deeper level of embodiment for physical, emotional and life skills. That’s why we run all-day camps during the school hols.

It’s a high-reward experience where kids trade screens for real interactive play, getting out of their heads and connecting with their whole being through pure, joyful movement.

This consistent time on the equipment helps young people move past the initial nerves of a new skill and into a state of flow.

At Easter Camp, students move through a whirlwind of aerial and acrobatic disciplines, they learn object manipulation skills and they always adore the flying trapeze and group acrobatics.

By staying for the full week, they learn much more than tricks. They build real resilience and lasting friendships. It is the kind of holiday memory that stays with them long after the school term starts again.

Spaces are limited to ensure every student gets individual coaching and time on the apparatus.
