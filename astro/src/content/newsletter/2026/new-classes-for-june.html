CIRCUS FOR ALL

INTO THE FESTIVAL SPIRIT WE GO!

Our first pop-up circus event for the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games Festival takes place at St Enoch Centre tomorrow 12pm-3pm, as part of Circus For All – a name that carries our firm belief in what's possible.

While the world’s elite athletes chase medals, records, and fractions of seconds that decide gold, we’ll be offering something different in the middle of the shopping flow: aerial, acro, and object manipulation for anyone who feels curious enough to stop and try. Just bodies in motion, discovering what they can do, without thinking about factors that limit participation.

For those of us who train circus regularly, this is familiar ground. We already know how it builds strength, confidence, coordination, and a kind of physical intelligence that stays with you long after class ends. This is about widening that doorway, and letting more people feel it for themselves.

We’re proud to be part of the Festival, and grateful to Glasgow Life and the Festival team for trusting us to bring circus into the heart of the city in this way.

If you’re nearby, come say hello. Or better still, step in and have a go.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

STRONGER IN THE AIR

AERIAL CONDITIONING LANDS ON DAYTIME SCHEDULE

Yara’s Circus Fitness Aerial Conditioning class has outgrown its evening slot. The Thursday has been packed, the demand kept building, and so many people asked for another chance to get in that we've made it the first Circus Fitness class to be on the daytime timetable too.

You can now catch it on Tuesdays from 10am-11am.

This is the class that builds the engine behind your aerial work: the grip that lasts longer, the shoulders that feel steadier, the core strength that suddenly makes tricks click into place instead of feeling like a wrestling match with gravity.

Even better, it’s strangely addictive. There’s something deeply satisfying about discovering you can hold longer, move cleaner, and feel more powerful in the air week by week.

Basically: less flailing, more flying.

Circus Fitness: Aerial Conditioning

Tuesdays 10am-11am, from 2nd June

Cost: £7.50 with an 8-class pass

Swing when you're winning

There’s more than the occasional moment in flying trapeze where everything flows beautifully…then turns into an energetic wrestle with the bar. Now Nea and Kirsty are bringing a new class if you want more flow. More power. More control in the air.

Tuesday Swing Technique & Cradle Basing class focuses on the details that change everything. The swing is the jet fuel for all tricks. It provides the power and the style.

Nea and Kirsty will coach you to stronger swings, smoother turn-arounds, cleaner returns to the platform, and the explosive timing that makes tricks really take off.

You’ll also dive into the different technique for swings on the cradle base – and try basing yourself, if you’re up for it.

It’s technical work – and the kind that makes flying feel bigger, lighter, and far more satisfying.

Swing Technique & Cradle Basing

Tuesdays, 8.30-10pm, from 2nd June

Cost: £17 with an 8-class pass or £25 drop-in
Tech time: book here

Unwind on Fridays

Amber’s Circus Fitness Contemporary Dance class is stretching into something new. It’s now a flexibility class – giving you another chance to open up tight shoulders, hips, backs, and hamstrings during the week.

With Freya’s Monday CF Flex class nearly always full, Amber’s class gives you more space to breathe, lengthen, and work on the mobility that makes circus feel better in your body.

That’s the whole idea behind Circus Fitness classes. They build the foundations that support everything else – strength, mobility, coordination, control, and body awareness. The hidden scaffolding behind the flashy stuff.

And there’s something glorious about walking out of a flexibility class feeling taller, looser, and less like a human pretzel that’s been left in a cupboard overnight.

Circus Fitness: Flexibility

Fridays 6pm-7pm, from 5th June

Cost: £7.50 with an 8-class pass

STRATOSPHERIC SPIN

ELKE'S NEXT-LEVEL FLY POLE WORKSHOPS

Last time Elke’s fly pole workshops sold out – and this time, she handed the decision-making over to Instagram and let the people choose what types of workshops they wanted. Now you have a full spread of spinny goodness to select, from dynamic tricks to flowing choreography.

Alongside the tricks workshops, there’s a new choreo-focused session for anyone who’s watched fly pole and thought: HOW are they doing that? Starting from the floor, Elke will guide you through flow, musicality, rhythm, transitions, and ways to weave the pole into choreography so it feels less like a piece of apparatus and more like a dance partner that occasionally tries to fling you into the stratosphere.

This choreo one is aimed at beginner to intermediate movers, with extra challenges available for those who want them.

For the other workshops, there’s room to level up too. If you’ve attended before and feel solid with the requirements for the next stage, go for it. And if you’d rather stay at the same level and deepen things instead, Elke has extra spicy variations ready and waiting.

Basically, there’s a workshop with your name on it.

Cost: £17 each with an 8-class pass or £25 drop-in

FOR YOUR EYES ONLY: THE SPRING BALL

TEEN CLASS SPY THRILLER THIS SUNDAY

This Sunday, our Youth Circus teens step into The Spring Ball – a world they’ve built themselves, thread by thread, choice by choice.

The music, the storyline, the characters and the tension beneath the surface have all been shaped by the group, with guidance from their instructors as the structure around their ideas takes form.

The Spring Ball is a spy thriller disguised as celebration, where nothing is quite as innocent as it first appears.

What begins as an elegant gathering gradually reveals a double life through doubles on aerial, solo moments, acting sequences, and physical storytelling.

It builds towards a tragic conclusion, and a final question that lingers after the last cue fades, perhaps as a life lesson?

Students from the other classes are welcome to join family and friends of the performers to enjoy this fabulous in-class showing.

The Spring Ball

Sunday 24th May | 1.20pm

Family and friends encouraged to come along!
