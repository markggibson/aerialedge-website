## GET PREPPED FOR THE BIG WEEKEND

WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW BEFORE EASTER EDGE

Hey Mark,

It's not long before we start the four-day funfest of The Easter Edge, so we thought you'd like some prep info.

Talk to us if you have any queries or concerns at all. We want this to be a fantastic four days for you, so don't keep things to yourself, we can probably help. You can email info@contact.aerialedge.co.uk or chat to us in the school.

1. Set yourself up for success

-

You might want to think about what goals to set for yourself in each of your tracks before you arrive, and consider bringing a notebook if that’s useful for you learn, or to track your progress.

-

If you're coming just for the fun and don't want to set goals, then enjoy the ride whatever way you choose!

-

If you're unsure what to aim for, you can set goals with your instructors. You may want to do that at the start of the session. Or if it's a new art for you, you may prefer to wai til you've had a session.

-

Pace yourself. Sure, push yourself, but training all day every day for four days can be intense, and we’re all about avoiding injuries.

2. Videos and sharing

Feel free to take videos and analyse them with your instructors – and share them on socials!

We'd love you tag us @aerial_edge. As well as proudly showing us and your friends what you can do, you'll be helping the mission to bring circus to as many people as possible.

Aerial Edge is a not-for-profit organisation, and social posts spread the word so they help us keep the doors open for you to train.

We will be in the school filming clips for our Instagram, website etc. When you signed up to train with us, you will have signed a disclaimer meaning that you give permission for us to do that. If you don't want us to film you, please email info@contact.aerialedge.co.uk before you arrive on Friday.

3. Timings and facilities

-

Please arrive by 9.30am and be ready to join the warm-up shortly afterwards.

-

The ⁠⁠first workshop is at 10am, and each session lasts 90min. There’s a 15min break morning and afternoon, and a 45min lunch break. There are food outlets including Asda, Aldi, Starbucks, McDonald's and others close enough for you to walk there and back at lunch, but you'd have to sprint to make it in a break. You might prefer to bring everything you need for the day with you.

-

⁠⁠There's a kettle and a bean-to-cup coffee machine in reception. Someone put ground coffee into it recently so it now needs specialist surgery – we're working on it, but it's only fair to warn you that it may still be dysfunctional by Friday, so it would be wise to bring your own supplies and/or recaffeinate with local outlets.

-

Please don't take cups or food into the training space. ⁠⁠The only liquid containers allowed outside of reception are water bottles with non-spill lids. Why? Because no one likes being stuck to the mats, or rolling around with the whiff of sour milk that's seeped into the cracks between them.

4. What to wear

-

There's still a winter chill in Glasgow so layer up – both outside and inside the school. The days will start cold and get warmer as training gets underway.

-

Layers are also required for Chinese pole training. Leggings under jeans would be ideal, and a long-sleeve T-shirt under another layer. You'll thank us when you get to grips with the pole. Rubber soled shoes will be your friends – something like Feiyue or old-fashioned plimsolls works much better than trainers on the pole.

-

Rings, watches, necklaces and suchlike can tear silks, hammock and other equipment so we kindly ask you not to wear them. Only soft materials can be worn on the trampoline – not belts, watches etc which can damage the bed. Safety comes first and lots of people will be disappointed if we have to take apparatus out of use during the weekend because it's been flawed.

-

Jewellery that you treasure is best left at home. It can get caught and broken on apparatus or get lost if you take it off. You'd be surprised what we have in our lost property box.

5. Have a chat

You can be added to the Easter Edge 2026 WhatsApp group, where we can share plans, pix, videos, joy and laughter. Just tap the link below which will open an email, and shoot us a note saying you want to join.

We finish at 5.15pm every day and are happy if you want to use the group to arrange drinks and/or food int he evenings with other participants in the festival.
