Hi Mark,

In case you aren't signed up to our weekly newsletter where we deliver all the updates, I want to let you know about performance opportunities for Youth Circus students.

After seeing some fantastic choreographed acts in our recent end-of-class showings, we knew some students were ready for a bigger stage. So now youth students have the opportunity to perform an act at our Open Rig Night, on Saturday 25th April. 

There will still be regular in-class showings, so there's absolutely no pressure for students to create an act for Open Rig Night. Everyone can do whatever they feel comfortable with.

The Open Rig Night debut

This is a proper show setting with professional lighting, music, and a friendly, supportive atmosphere. Our last event had an audience of 100 people there to support their friends and family, making it a brilliant prospect for young performers.

The youth students will open the show at 6.30pm, with the adult performances starting around 7.30pm. Students who would like to perform an act should speak with their instructor and fill in our online form. We will be sending students home with a paper note containing all the specific details needed for this. 

Tickets will be available through our ticket link or via the button at the top of the Aerial Edge website. Everyone is welcome – for students who are not performing, it's a lovely chance to be inspired and to encourage classmates who are on stage. 

In-class performance showings

We also have opportunities for you to come in and see samples of what students have been learning. Please come to these classes at the times below – we've made it the last 15 minutes before pick-up time:

  • Sunday 29th March: 7–12 year-olds at 11.15am and at 12.45pm

  • Wednesday 1st April: 7-18 year-olds at 6.45pm

  • Sunday 26th April – Teen spy tragicomedy at 1.20pm

Everyone's excited to show you what they've been working on, so come with friends and family ready to cheer them on.

See you soon,

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge, Glasgow's Circus School
