HIT THE YEAR MOVING FASTER

MAKE IT DYNAMIC TO MAKE IT BETTER

Kick off the year by getting some oomph into your movement. Freya’s dynamics workshops are all about finding momentum and making it work for you.

And on the subject of momentum, keep it going – if there are enough people keen to carry on, Freya's up for teaching a Dynamics 2.0 workshop at the end of the month.

Once movement is set in motion, it carries you further than effort alone ever could.

Dynamic Rope explores beats, turns, hand-slide transitions, simple releases and punchy combinations. You’ll focus on timing, flow and efficiency, so sequences feel smoother, lighter and more connected. Less muscling. More movement doing the work.

Dynamic Hoop takes familiar shapes and sets them in motion. Expect refined beats, alternate grips, dynamic switches, drops and satisfying ways of turning exits into confident entries. Every action leads naturally into the next – momentum becomes your guide.

These workshops are designed for Improver / Intermediate students and anyone ready to expand their aerial range. The principles translate beyond rope and hoop into flying trapeze and other aerial disciplines – making this a great way to kick-start your training after the holidays.

Freya teaches on our Foundation and Professional Development programmes and is known for making dynamic work feel playful, clear and confidence-building. You’ll leave with tools you can keep using – momentum that lasts beyond the workshop.

RUN AWAY TO THE CIRCUS

(AND BE BACK BY MONDAY)

Feeling the daily grind slump before you even go back to work?

We know the dream…running away to join the circus. But we also know the reality…a mortgage, meetings, and a life that doesn’t just pause. You want to dive into the deep end of full-time circus training, but you can’t necessarily take four weeks let alone four months out.

Introducing Intensives for 1, 2 or 3 weeks

These are our new bite-sized Intensives, designed for those who want the full-time experience without giving up the day job.

Think of it as a retreat with purpose. You’ll step out of your daily grind and into the shoes of a full-time student, gaining the same coaching excellence as our professional programmes, condensed into a timeframe that fits your annual leave.

Why hit the master reset button?

• Compounded Progress: When you train daily, you don't have time to "forget" a skill or lose your momentum between sessions. Each day builds directly on the last, allowing you to smash through plateaus that could otherwise take months to move.

• A Sanctuary of Movement: This is protected time for your dreams and goals. It’s a dedicated window to focus wholeheartedly on what you can achieve.

• The Full-Time Feel: You’ll train alongside our full-time students on longer courses who come from throughout the UK, Europe and beyond for the world-class training, and experience the buzz of the breakthroughs together.

Go back like a new person

You won’t just return to your day job with a few new tricks, you’ll have a recharged perspective to offer, a body and mind that feel alive, and your creativity will be re-awakened.

When you invest in your own playful spirit and potential, you bring a more energised, capable version of yourself back to your colleagues, your friends, and your family.

Stop waiting for someday. Book it in for 2026.
