THE PLANUARY PRINCIPLE

FIND YOUR SUSTAINABLE MOMENTUM

Congratulations if you’re sticking with your new year resolutions, you’ve passed the second Friday in January, the milestone when many fall by the wayside. Whether or not your "new year, new me" energy is giving way to the siren call of the sofa, this is a good time to figure out how to find your sustainable momentum.

Resolutions built on deprivation – like giving up alcohol or certain foods – can feel harder than pushing water uphill, especially in the depths of winter.

We prefer revolutions to resolutions. Less about doing less and more about doing more that makes you feel alive. A revolution takes you out of your comfort zone because it’s worth it. You’re preparing the way for better days.

I’m talking about circus training, of course. We like new things in a new year as much as anyone – that’s one reason why we’ve radically shaken up our courses and open training options to make them easier for you to enjoy.

So, sure, use the impetus of a brand new year full of promise and hope. But instead hitting the ground running out of blood sugar straight after a fortnight of feasting, you could plan how and when you can do a Circus Intensive, from one day to four weeks.

You’re more likely to find the “new me” with a well-planned programme of activities that you love. Check out the options below.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

HIBERNATION IS FOR BEARS

WAKE UP YOUR WINTER

Hard as it is to accept, humans aren’t designed for hibernation. But boy, do we ever need some ways to wake up our winter. Well, hey, we have more than one!

• The one-day intensive: This is a short-burst tactical strike on sleepiness. This pass gives you access to any and every class on any weekday, for all the hours that we are open. Treat it like a pick & mix or do the whole lot – that’ll definitely warm you up!

• The cheeky wee weeks: These are the Great Escapes that we’ve just invented. Not everyone can take four weeks or four months out to do one of our flagship courses, so now you can take it one week at a time. Our Intensives are for one to four weeks. You join the classes that are scheduled for the longer-term students, 10am-5pm.

• The Fabulous February: This is the best deal ever. It’s the same price as a four-week Intensive, but you also get access to all the classes on the evening schedule. This is about choices – doing a class in every time slot on the schedule would be a special kind of madness.

All our prices are extraordinary value – and we work on the principle that the longer you stay, the deeper the discount.

LAND OF ROPE AND GLORY

THE FOUR-MONTH TRANSFORMATION

What does it actually look like to commit to circus for four months, or a year? You get a glimpse from the clip above taken from the showreel of our four-month Foundation Course graduate, Sarah. She started with curiosity and ended with a professionally filmed act that captures her technical progress and her unique artistic style.

This course is a carefully designed dive into the technical, physical, and creative aspects of circus.

It’s structured to first let you explore how you feel about a wide range of arts. Some people arrive with a preference for one, and develop another as a favourite.

Graduation means an ensemble show, working with a professional director. To go straight there without performance experience would not be setting you up for something beyond success: an optimal moment in your life.

So we get you to a first milestone, by coaching you to create a solo or duo act for Open Rig Night, our twice-yearly cabaret-style evening of circus entertainment.

That’s a friendly and appreciative audience of up to 100 people who know they are there to support you, they’re not a tough crowd in a nightclub, they are friends, family and fellow circus beings. There’s always lots of applause and cheering at Open Rig Nights.

All that is fabulous, and more than that, being on a full-time course gives you more than physical strengths. The journey with your classmates brings up personal challenges and brings out character strengths. You come to understand yourself better, and overcome limiting beliefs.

How does all this happen? Because it’s fantastic fun. The Aerial Edge vibe carries encouragement, support and laughter all the way through.

That’s why people cross oceans to learn with us. When are you coming?

SPARK THE SUNDAY REVOLUTION

THERE'S NO BETTER WAY TO SPEND YOUR DAY

Question. What are you doing with your Sunday afternoons that could possibly be better than a double flying trapeze session with Hamish?!

If you live far from Aerial Edge, there's plenty of time before and after to travel. It's worth it for three hours at the bar. But remember there are two classes on Fridays too so you could make a weekend of it.

If you live closer, you're perfectly placed to enjoy a lie-in and a lazy brunch then delegate the construction of the traditional Sunday dinner to someone – anyone, including the least friendly pub landlord in your area – before arriving for the warm-up at 1pm.

Should the inhabitants of your household stage a revolution over the roast, you could go back to oversee operations after the first class. But you'd be depriving them of a valuable learning opportunity.

Having worked out their unjustified anger along with the temperament of the oven and other members of the household, they may achieve a level of satisfaction potentially on a par with yours, as you return from the second fly class with ample time for a gin and tonic before adjusting the seasoning and sitting down to a well-earned meal.

And should you be the sole occupant of your property, well, you are in the fortunate position of being able to do whatever pleases you, before a multitude of dinner choices.

It's impossible to understand why anyone would do anything else, really.
