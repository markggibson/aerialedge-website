SHORTCUT TO YOUR NEXT LEVEL

HOW THE EDGE IS A PRESSURE COOKER FOR PROGRESS

There are less than 10 spaces left for Easter Edge – here are eight great reasons why one of them should be yours.

There is a fundamental difference between practicing an art and living it. For most of the year, we fit our training into the gaps between work, family, and the thousand other commitments that pull at our attention. We make progress, certainly, but it is often a gentle climb.

An intensive is a different beast entirely. It is a dedicated pocket of time where the outside world recedes, leaving only the equipment, the community, and the movement. It is a rare opportunity to stop "fitting training in" and instead allow yourself to be fully consumed by it.

Stepping into an immersive environment like this does something to the brain and the body that a weekly class simply cannot replicate. It’s a pressure cooker for progress.

Here are eight great reasons why you should snap up your spot before the track booking opens next Saturday:

1. The alchemy of the intensive – Training once a week is like sipping water, an intensive is like diving into a river. You get swept along in the flow towards real, permanent breakthroughs.

2. Muscle up your memory – When you train for multiple days in a row, your muscle memory doesn't have time to fade. You bypass the "re-remembering" phase of weekly class and start every session at your previous day’s peak. It is the fastest way to climb upwards from a plateau.

3. The safety of the collective – There is a specific kind of bravery that only exists in a room full of people who want you to succeed. Our community acts like a literal and metaphorical safety mat, providing the encouragement you need to try that drop or nail that sequence that feels "too big" when you are training alone or with different people in sessions with days between them.

4. Ditch the solo struggle – It can be tough to find motivation in a quiet room. Move away from the isolation of solo practice toward the electric energy of a community that refuses to let you fail.

5. Rewire your limits – Your brain is an instrument that needs tuning. Learning in this high-frequency environment moves you away from old "I can't" narratives and toward a mindset where you believe that the achievement of new moves is a matter of practice not ability.

6. The catapult effect – Intensive training is like stretching a slingshot. By pulling back from your daily routine and focusing purely on your craft, you build the tension needed to catapult your skills further in four days than you would in four months.

7. The compounding shortcut – It can take months trying to decode complex mechanics on your own, or in weekly sessions. Much like interest in a bank account, an intensive makes your progress grow exponentially. While weekly sessions are incremental savings, four days of continuous real-time refinement with our instructors means each new layer of technique builds on the one before it. You bypass the frustration of ingrained bad habits and watch your skill "balance" skyrocket in a fraction of the time.

8. The finish line feeling – Walking out of the final session of an intensive is like finishing a marathon you didn't know you could run. You leave with a re-calibrated sense of what your body is capable of, toward a version of yourself that is stronger, more capable, and open to inspiration.

LEARN FROM AN INTERNATIONAL STAR

ELEVATE WITH ELKE

When an artist of Elke’s calibre teaches in our school, it offers a rare shortcut to mastery that usually requires long-term trial and error. So it’s no wonder that all four of her workshops are nearly full already.

As a world-class specialist in flying pole, she brings a deep understanding of the precise physics and refined body mechanics needed to command such a demanding apparatus.

A bridge between worlds

If you are coming from an aerial background, you will find a familiar sense of flight, but with the added structural support of the pole. If you are a pole dancer, this is your chance to shed the floor and take your sequences into the vertical space. Elke’s expert guidance ensures you build the specific conditioning needed to handle the centrifugal force with grace and control.

The magic of momentum

Learning from a specialist like Elke allows you to move away from static transitions and toward a style defined by fluid, cinematic momentum. Because the pole is suspended, it responds to your weight and movement, creating a partnership between the artist and the apparatus. These workshops will help you find that sweet spot where gravity feels like an option rather than a rule.

TAKE YOUR MOMENT

BE CENTRE STAGE AT OPEN RIG NIGHT

There is a special kind of magic that happens when the house lights go down and the spotlight finds you in the air. On Saturday 25th April, we are opening the floor (and the rigging) for Open Rig Night – a high-energy, low-pressure evening designed for one thing: celebrating what you’ve been building.

This time, we’re expecting more of our fabulous Youth Circus students to take part. At their last in-class showings, it was clear that many are ready for a new performance experience, so it will be exciting for them, their families and us for them to open the show.

This time you shine

Whether you have a polished routine or a sequence you’re just starting to love, we want to see it. And so do other people! This is your chance to move away from the "maybe one day" mindset and toward the thrill of a live audience.

We’ll kick things off at 6:30pm with our incredible youth students, flowing into the adult performances from 7:30pm onwards.

Support every step

If the idea of performing feels a bit daunting, don’t let it hold you back. Our registration form is designed to guide you, prompting you to think about the flow and feel of your act.

If you find yourself stuck or need a creative spark to finish a transition, just ask your instructor, they’ll be delighted to offer hints, tips and creative ideas for your act. We are here to help you move away from hesitation and toward a performance you’re proud of.

Nail the tech

To ensure your moment in the spotlight is seamless, we need to talk tech. To move away from the stress of a silent stage, please remember the following music rules:

  • • Upload a high-quality mp3: Please avoid screenshots or links. We want to make sure we have the exact version you’ve been practicing to. You can download an mp3 file from Amazon, Apple Music and other places.

  • • No Spotify links: We can’t rely on the internet gods when you’re mid-climb. A hard file on our system ensures the music never cuts out.

  • • Edit your track: If you want your music to start at the 30-second mark, please edit the file so it begins exactly where you want it. This ensures your timing is perfect from the first beat. We’ll play all mp3 files from the beginning.
