MOVEMENT FROM FLOOR TO AIR

EXPANDING YOUR AERIAL VOCABULARY

There are many secrets to making your aerial moves look effortless, and one of them is floorwork. This is why dance class is a core part of our full-time courses, and a pillar of our circus fitness programme, which gives you solid foundations for aerial and acrobatic arts.

It is easy to think of dance and aerial as two separate worlds, but in Amber’s class you learn the many ways that moves can be expressed on the floor and in the air.

Whether it’s a specific way of engaging your core, a fluid roll, or a transition that builds momentum, these are the fundamental building blocks of aerial greatness.

In the clip above, you can see the shapes Amber makes on the floor being translated by Freya on the hoop. By practicing these patterns on the ground, you are building the muscle memory and repertoire you need to be more creative when you’re in the air.

Plus, Amber’s class is a whole load of fun in its own right.

If you’re thinking about stepping into the spotlight for an open rig night, Amber’s class gives you:

  • Creative transitions – move beyond simply walking up to your apparatus, learn how to add performance to your entrance.

  • Expanded vocabulary – discover new ways to link tricks together that feel fluid rather than functional.

  • Solid foundations – as part of the circus fitness family, this class builds the strength and grace that supports every other circus art.

Whether you want to refine your act or you just want a class that is a great workout in its own right, learning to move on the floor will completely change how you feel in the air.

WATCH THE MAGIC LAND

WHY NOT JOIN US FOR OPEN RIG NIGHT

On the average weekday evening, the school is a brightly lit noisy hive of all sorts of activities, with students throwing themselves into their favourite tricks and stretching out sore muscles. But on special Saturdays, a spotlight cuts through the quiet darkness to pick out a single figure who has stopped practicing and started storytelling – and just like that, the show has begun.

On show nights, the rig is transformed into a dramatically lit stage, where the imaginations of the performers and the audience meet in mutual delight. It’s a shared experience that is nevertheless unique to everyone present. What you see is what you take from it.

This is when the repetition of the training sessions turns into a stage full of dreams coming true in real time.

Open Rig Night is the next show, it's an invitation to see what happens when intention meets creativity. You will see everything from experimental sequences to polished acts that have been months in the making. It is a night of high-flying risk, supportive cheers, and the kind of atmosphere that only happens when someone finds their edge in front of an appreciative audience.

Why you should be there...

  • See courage in action – watch how the lessons from all sorts of classes translate into breathtaking performance.

  • Support the community – your cheers are the fuel that helps our students push past their nerves, to soar.

  • Find your own inspiration – there is no better way to get motivated for anything than seeing what is possible when you give it your best effort.

As a non–profit organisation, every ticket sold goes straight back into our world-class coaching, maintaining our equipment and – less excitingly – into paying the whacking rent and utility bills.

And this is the best-value night out in town. So take a seat, bring a friend, and be inspired by the people who make Aerial Edge so special.

SPRING INTO ACTION

5 REASONS TO COME TO EASTER CAMP

Looking for a way to peel the kids away from their screens without creating a rebellious riot? Easter circus camp is the answer.

The school is a giant playground where kids don't just burn off energy, they learn life skills along with circus. From the adrenaline of the flying trapeze to the focus of juggling and the teamwork of acrobatics, it’s non–stop movement that builds resilience without them even realising they are working hard.

We swap the usual holiday boredom for a high–energy week of flying, flipping, and finding new confidence.

Here’s why a circus break is the ultimate holiday treat, whether they join us for a single day or the full week.

  • The click moment – When you’re doing circus all day, your body and brain get together in the zone where so much more is possible. There is nothing quite like the look on a student’s face when they finally nail something that felt out of reach.

  • Finding their circus squad – Even in a single day, the vibe is so infused with enthusiasm that students cheer each other on, solve puzzles together, and become part of a team of performers. It is a great way to make new friends who love the same moves. It’s also a great moment to bring a friend along and make them feel immediately part of the crowd.

  • A massive confidence boost – Watching a child go from "I can't do that" in the morning to "Watch this!" by pick-up time is the best part of our week. That new-found bravery doesn't just stay in the school – it goes home with them.

  • Creating an act – The more days they’re with us, the more chance that they can move beyond just following instructions. They experiment with their favourite and ways to move, figuring out how to link tricks together into a sequence that feels like natural movement to them, so they can truly call their own. And they can show you when you come in for the informal showing on Friday afternoon.

  • The ultimate energy burner – Instead of the holiday sofa-slump, they get a day (or five!) of high-energy, fully physical fun, which feels fantastic for so many who are usually in their heads all day either studying or doing something on screens. They will come home tired out in the best possible way – full of stories, new skills, and a lot of circus spirit.

You can book the full five-day experience to see the biggest transformation (and discount), or simply pick the individual days that suit your schedule.
