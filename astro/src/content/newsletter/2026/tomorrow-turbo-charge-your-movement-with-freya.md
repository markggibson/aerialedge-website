## THE CENTRE OF GRAVITY

ALL ROADS LEAD TO OUR RIG

There is an international energy in the school this week. It is one thing to call yourself world-class, and it’s another thing entirely to see it in action.

This week we welcomed former Foundation Course students who travelled from the USA, Europe, and all over the UK just to come back and train together at Aerial Edge. Next week the Professional Development Course returns with more international students.

All this and the wealth of instructors from across the world have turned the school into a global hub, proving that circus creates a community that transcends borders.

This all-in spirit is at the heart of all our new year new options  – the Open Training memberships that we created for these former students, our Circus “Spa Day”, the  short-burst Intensive Courses and the All Access Pass for February for people ready to move in and live the circus life.

Whether you’re crossing an ocean or just crossing the street to get here, you’re very welcome.

Mark Gibson

Aerial Edge Founder

## NEW YEAR, HIGHER GEAR

RARE CHANCE TO TURBO CHARGE YOUR MECHANICS

Tomorrow is a rare chance to lift the hood and install a turbo-charger into your aerial engine. Freya’s dynamic techniques aren't a standard tune-up – they are the secret mechanics that turn strain into effortless power.

Learning these techniques in Freya’s hoop and rope workshops tomorrow is like adding power steering to a vintage car. Suddenly, you aren't wrestling with the equipment – you are gliding with it.

You can travel further, faster, and with way more style. It is the difference between being stuck in rush-hour traffic and being a Formula 1 Ferrari screaming through the streets of Monaco.

Whether you want a smoother ride or a total gear change in your technique, Freya will help you find that high-speed flow.

And if you're up for it, a Dynamics 2.0 workshop is a possibility at the end of the – to keep driving forward!

Don’t you want a body that understands how to handle the curves and shifts of momentum?

## TAKE A BREAK FROM THE DAY JOB

A WEEK OR TWO AT CIRCUS COULD CHANGE YOUR LIFE

There is a specific kind of magic that happens when you stop being a visitor and start becoming a resident at Aerial Edge.

Our new short-term Circus Intensives are designed for those who want to do more than just drop in for evening classes – they are for people who want to escape their day job for a week or more, and relocate their daily life to the air and the mats.

It is a fantastic chance to see how far you can go when the "real” world is put on pause.

Circus brain

Choosing a one to four-week intensive is beyond training; it’s immersion. When you are here from 10am to 5pm, your "circus brain" takes over. You start to see the world through the lens of physics, momentum, and creative play. It can be life-changing, frankly.

And there's a mega-deal available for February – the exclusive All Access Pass means you can do any class from the day and evening schedule for the entire month for the bargain price of £795.

Tangible outcomes

If you’re looking for more tangible outcomes, our four-month Foundation Course starts in March.

This is the course that takes you from enthusiast to artist. It is a comprehensive build of your technical engine and your creative heart. You start training a broad range of circus arts to find your favourites, then develop a solo act for Open Rig Night. After that, you become an integral part of the creation of an ensemble show with a professional director.

You’ll have to be quick to join the March Foundation Course, it’s nearly full (there’s another starting in August). If you’ve been waiting for a sign to change your coordinates, this is it.

## THE NEXT STAGE FOR YOUTH CIRCUS

BIGGER NIGHTS AND BRIGHTER LIGHTS

Following our fantastic pre-Christmas youth performances, it became clear that the best way for our young artists to evolve is to integrate act creation directly into our core training, and launch a dedicated Open Rig Night.

We are evolving the way we teach performance skills to make it more accessible and exciting for everyone.

The separate Performance Programme is taking a bow to make way for a more streamlined approach. From now on, the two Wednesday classes will be merged and performance coaching will be built right into the class at 5.30pm.

Students who want to focus on the spotlight will get tuition in choreography and characterisation, preparing them for their very own Youth Open Rig Nights.

These nights will offer the full professional experience – staging, lighting, and production. Students can perform solo or duo acts when they feel ready. For those who prefer a lower-pressure approach, our informal end-of-term showings will continue to be the perfect place to share skills with family and friends.

This pathway isn't just for Wednesdays; our Sunday students are also encouraged to prepare acts for the Open Rig Nights if they have the performance itch. Whether you want to master a move or tell a story in the air, the stage is set.
