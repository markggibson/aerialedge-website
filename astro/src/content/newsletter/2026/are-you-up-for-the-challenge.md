## TIPS TO HELP YOU TAKE THE LEAP

YOU CAN DO IT, WE KNOW YOU CAN

Moving from a regular class to the Open Rig Night stage is a leap, and it is completely natural to wonder if you are ready.  But it's really this simple: if you have a sequence you love, you are ready to show it.

This night is specifically designed as a welcoming space. If you have been on the fence about signing up, remember that the goal isn't perfection, it’s about the thrill of the try.

The most memorable performances aren't always the most technical ones, they are the ones where the performer is clearly having the time of their life.

The transition from student to performer can be just a matter of perspective. You have skills from your training, and the open rig is simply a way to share them.

Here’s some tips to help you take that step up to the stage:

-

Trust your muscle memory – your body knows these moves better than your nervous brain does. Rehearse your act, then let your training take over once the music starts.

-

Focus on the feeling – instead of worrying about every technical detail, focus on the joy of being in the air, because that’s what will shine through to the audience.

-

Embrace the butterflies – that nervous energy is actually a powerful tool that can help you stay sharp and present during your set.

-

Lean on the community – you aren't out there alone, you are surrounded by coaches and fellow students who are all rooting for your success.

Your instructor will give you all the support you need to pull this off. Ask them for help with your act, and take a step towards a moment you'll never forget. Please register using the form below by Friday 17th April.

## Turn your week upside down

Forget the standard holiday club and let them fly instead. Our Easter camps are designed for kids who want to spend their break upside down, on aerial apparatus or the floor.

It’s a high-energy experience where they can trade screens for the spotlight, get out of their heads and connect with their whole being.

They will move through a whirlwind of disciplines, from acrobatics to aerials, flying trapeze to juggling, building real physical confidence and making friends along the way. It is the kind of holiday memory that stays with them long after the school term starts again.

Spaces are limited to ensure every student gets plenty of time on the equipment and individual coaching. It is the perfect opportunity for them to dive deep into the circus arts and come home with a brand-new set of skills. Give them a break they will actually talk about.
