Hi Mark,

In case you aren't signed up to our weekly newsletter where we deliver all the updates, I want to let you know about performance opportunities for Youth Circus students.

After seeing some fantastic choreographed acts in our recent end-of-class showings, we knew some students were ready for a bigger stage. So now youth students have the opportunity to perform an act at our Open Rig Night, on Saturday 25th April.

There will still be regular in-class showings, so there's absolutely no pressure for students to create an act for Open Rig Night. Everyone can do whatever they feel comfortable with.

## The Open Rig Night debut

This is a proper show setting with professional lighting, music, and a friendly, supportive atmosphere. Our last event had an audience of 100 people there to support their friends and family, making it a brilliant prospect for  young performers.

The youth students will open the show at 6.30pm, with the adult performances starting around 7.30pm.
