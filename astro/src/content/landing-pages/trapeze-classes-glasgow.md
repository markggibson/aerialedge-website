**Fly, swing and find your strength on the trapeze — at Glasgow's circus school.**

Trapeze is the most exhilarating way to get fit you'll ever try. You don't need any experience, and you don't need to be strong or flexible to start — our instructors take complete beginners through every step, safely. Most people surprise themselves in their very first class.

Aerial Edge is a not-for-profit circus school in Glasgow with students from age one to people in their seventies. Whatever your starting point, there's a place for you in the air.

##### Two open-level trapeze classes to book

We have two trapeze classes with space for new faces right now. Both are **open level** — beginners welcome, and experienced flyers will be pushed too.

**Static Trapeze — Wednesdays, 7:00pm**
Learn balances, drops and shapes on the still bar with Freya Morton. A great place to start if you want to build trapeze skills at your own pace.

<a href="https://goteamup.com/p/3751555-aerial-edge/" target="_blank" class="button small">Book Static Trapeze (Wed 7pm)</a>

**Flying Trapeze — Mondays, 8:30pm**
The real thing — leave the platform, swing out, and catch air. Taught by Mark Gibson and the team. No experience needed; the safety lines do the worrying for you.

<a href="https://goteamup.com/p/3751555-aerial-edge/" target="_blank" class="button small">Book Flying Trapeze (Mon 8:30pm)</a>
