**Get strong and find your balance on static trapeze – at Glasgow's circus school.**

Static trapeze is one of the most rewarding ways to get fit you'll ever try, and it's a good starting point in aerial because there's something to sit on, and hold onto – the ropes that it hangs from. It feels like a childhood swing when you first get to grips with it, familiar in a way other apparatus aren't.

But even in your first class you can surprise yourself by doing things on it that you could never imagine as a kid on a swing – tricks standing or sitting on the bar, hanging below it, and on the ropes themselves, with endless combinations. Stick with it and drops come next, alongside the balances and shapes. You fall out of one position and stop, under control, in another. This is an entirely different art to flying trapeze, with its own skills and challenges, and you can train solo or try doubles moves with other people in the class.

You don't need any experience, and you don't need to be strong or flexible to start. Our instructors take complete beginners through every step, safely.

Aerial Edge is a not-for-profit circus school in Glasgow, training students from age one to people in their seventies. Whatever your starting point, there's a place for you in the air.
