**Climb, wrap and drop on aerial rope — at Glasgow's circus school.**

Aerial rope (corde lisse) is a single soft rope you climb, wrap into, and drop from. It's a powerful, dramatic apparatus — and one of the most satisfying ways to build real, functional strength. You don't need to be strong to start; you get strong by doing it, and our instructors take complete beginners through it safely.

Aerial Edge is a not-for-profit circus school in Glasgow with students from age one to people in their seventies. Whatever your starting point, there's a place for you in the air.

##### The class to book

**Aerial Rope — Tuesdays, 7:00pm**
An open-level class with Nea Kunnari — beginners welcome, and improvers will be pushed too. Learn to climb cleanly, wrap securely, and drop with control.
