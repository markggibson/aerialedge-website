**Climb, wrap and drop on aerial silks — at Glasgow's circus school.**

Aerial silks (sometimes called tissu) is two lengths of fabric you climb, wrap into, and drop from. It looks impossible and feels incredible — and you build the strength to do it as you go. No experience or special fitness needed to start; our instructors guide you safely through every wrap and climb.

Aerial Edge is a not-for-profit circus school in Glasgow with students from age one to people in their seventies. Whatever your starting point, there's a place for you in the air.
