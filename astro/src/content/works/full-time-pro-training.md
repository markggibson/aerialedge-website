On this page you’ll get an overview of our programmes, with links to a dedicated page for each of them.

You get a high-quality programme for the lowest possible price at Aerial Edge, with plenty of fun and laughter included! We charge as little as we can, because that way more people can fulfil their circus dreams.

You’ll emerge from our courses with a strong skillset in circus techniques – and not just that. Challenges inevitably crop up as you train, and that’s good! We’re big on developing people through circus. We’ll help you to learn through these experiences, whether they’re physical, emotional or mental obstacles.

Whatever level you’re at when you join, we’ll make sure you make great progress from there. That means when you finish your course, you could do it all over again! Each time you’d make it your own, and you’d continue to build on your success.

You’re in good hands at Aerial Edge. We have decades of high-level expertise in circus arts and the global industry of creating education packages. People love our supportive and encouraging atmosphere – your instructors are there for you all the way. Come and join us!

## 1-year Professional Development Course

If you’ve already got circus skills and want to move towards professional level, this is a great route.
Maybe you don’t have a proper act, or you want to improve your technique to gain performance capability, or you want a bridge to a degree course.

Or perhaps you’ve done our Foundation Course and want to immerse yourself for a year in the friendly, empathic and supportive atmosphere! That’s why this course was created.

You focus on your chosen arts while still training others. You make huge leaps in performance. You get to grips with essential documentation. And you get a pro showreel!

More info on the One-Year Professional Development Course [here](/1-year-pdc/)

## Four-Month Foundation Course in Circus Arts
This is like no other Foundation Course. Our unique approach means you can gain solid skills in a broad range of arts, and life skills along the way

Plus you get an amazing show reel, filmed by our cinematographer Gabriel Stella! Use it with applications for courses, funding or work.

You’ll create a solo act and work with a professional Director to perform in an ensemble show.

More info on the Four-Month Full-Time Foundation Course [here](/foundation-course/).

## Four-Week Circus Intensive Courses
Like the Foundation Course, this gives you a broad range of circus disciplines. You can create a solo act and perform it if you choose.

It’s a great way to discover if full-time training is for you. It’s also good if you can’t commit to a whole four months, but you could free up four weeks of your life to dedicate yourself to circus and see where that takes you.

Warning: People sometimes can’t bear to leave so they stay on to finish the Foundation Course!


More info on the Four-Week Circus Intensive Course [here](/four-week-intensive/)

## Pro-Track Training
This programme is a selection of classes and self-training sessions from our daytime and evening schedules, curated specially for you.

It’s particularly suitable if you’re a circus professional, you want to become one, or you want to train like a pro.

We discuss your aims to understand what you need to learn and develop. Then we check your availability against our schedule, and your budget.

More on Pro-Track Training [here](/protrack/)

## Easter Edge Circus Festival
This is a fantastic four full days giving you 24 hours of progress-driven training with the Aerial Edge team and specialist guest instructors.

You choose four arts to focus on over the four days. Each pathway has a 90-minute session in that art every day, for four days.

People make appreciable improvements because everyone is moving together through the experience, and some arts improve technique in others.

More about Easter Edge [here](/easter-edge/)
