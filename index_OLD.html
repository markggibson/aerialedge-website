Developing people

Developing people through circus is what we do best.

We start where you are now and build from there. This is something anyone can do, regardless of age, body type, experience or other factors. Circus is for everybody, and we find that along with learning circus skills, people learn a lot about themselves too.

How we work

Whether you want to drop in for a single class or join one of our programmes, we have something for you. If you don't know where to start, come for a circus fitness class and you get to try everything! If you've always wanted to try silks or trapeze, sign up for a beginners class.

Adults from complete newbies to professional performers can have a blast and learn something new here. No commitment issues – to make everything as accessible as possible, you can enjoy yourself one date at a time in drop-in classes. But when you’re in love, you’re hooked, so you can also buy packs of classes at reduced rates, and/or join one of our programmes.

Youth Circus is for anyone under 18 years old. Classes are booked one term at a time and cover a multitude of circus disciplines to give you a broad range of skills and allow you to discover where your talents lie.

If you want to develop your ability or become a professional, come and join one of our part-time or full-time programmes. Check out our new timetable, we'll be adding new classes each month.
